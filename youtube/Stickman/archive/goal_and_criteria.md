1. **High RPM + High views** — best case, rare

3. **Low RPM + Exceptionally high views** — viable with Hindi/viral content

## Production Constraints (What Priyanshu CAN do)

- ✅ Nano Banana 2 image generation (one image at a time, consistent style)

- ✅ Explainer-style stickman (NOT full animation like cartoons)
- ✅ FFmpeg assembly + CapCut captions
- ✅ ElevenLabs voiceover

- ❌ Cannot do live action or real footage

## What the visual style looks like

- AI-generated images (Nano Banana 2) displayed in rapid sequence
- Same character/scene continues across images — action progresses
- Combined with voiceover narration
- Feels like a video but is technically a fast slideshow with motion
- Can reference animated stickman channels for IDEAS but execute in image-sequence style
