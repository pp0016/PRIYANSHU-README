## COUNTER (Anti-Sycophancy Check)

This plan assumes you can go from zero to polished script in 5 working days. That's aggressive.

**What could go wrong:**
1. Day 1 competitor research could take longer than expected if you get pulled into watching full videos instead of just noting stats. **Fix**: Set a timer. 5 channels = 5 entries. Don't watch full videos yet.
2. Day 5 script writing could be harder if you don't have a strong topic from Day 3. **Fix**: If Day 3 topic feels forced, sleep on it and finalize in the morning of Day 5 before writing.
3. The Vyro clip tasks (Day 3, Day 7) assume you know how to clip. If you've never clipped before, your first clip will take 2-3x longer. **Fix**: Pick the simplest possible clip for your first submission. 30 seconds, basic cut, no effects. Ship ugly, learn from feedback.

**If you're behind by Day 4 (Monday), don't panic.** The plan is designed so that Days 5-7 can absorb delays from Days 1-3. Day 4 is specifically there to reset.
