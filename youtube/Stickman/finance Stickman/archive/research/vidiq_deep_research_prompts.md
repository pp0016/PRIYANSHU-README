NO talking-head channels. NO face-cam creators.

Start directly with the table:
| Channel Name | Subs | Videos | Visual Format | Top Outlier Video Title | Outlier Views | VPH or View/Sub Ratio | Main Topic Angle |

After the table:
1. Which 3 channels are growing fastest right now and why?
2. What content gap exists that none of them are filling?

**What this does:** Finds the small hungry channels (5k–50k) that are breaking out RIGHT NOW in faceless finance. These are your direct competitors and the ones proving what topics work at low sub counts. The "growing fastest" question forces VidIQ to surface velocity data, not just size.
