# 📁 research/ — Competitive Intelligence & Macro Market Database

## 🎯 Purpose
Serves as the channel's foundational research archive, housing competitor performance metrics, keyword demand tables, and niche white spaces.

## ✅ What is REQUIRED Here
1. **`channel_benchmarks.md`**:
   - Tracking data for Tier S channels (*Quiet Money Stories*, *POV Wealth*, *Ollie Finance*, *Dark Ledger*).
   - Monthly subscriber growth rates, outlier breakout multiples, and views-per-subscriber ratios.
2. **`keyword_database.md`**:
   - Low-competition ($\le 35$), high-volume Tier-1 keyword inventory (`financial habits`, `frugal living`, `how pawn shops make money`, `stealth wealth`).
3. **`outlier_forensics.md`**:
   - Analysis of top 15+ viral videos in the niche and reverse-engineered packaging mechanics.

## ❌ What is NOT REQUIRED / FORBIDDEN
- Do NOT store raw daily script notes or temporary brainstorm drafts here.
- Do NOT track dead niches or collapsing topics (e.g. generic savings tips).
