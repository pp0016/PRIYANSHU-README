# 4. Fresh data pull: methodology and verification notes

For this expansion, every channel was re-pulled live rather than quoted from the earlier session. Tools used:

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `get_channel_stats` (with recent + top videos) | Live subscriber counts, lifetime views, video counts, per-video views/likes/comments/views-per-day |
| `search_youtube_channels` | Channel discovery by niche query, size band (5,000–50,000), faceless flag, and handle match |
| `find_outlier_videos` | Outlier/breakout detection scoped to channel IDs, plus global discovery queries |

**Discovery queries run for this expansion:**
- "money psychology animated stickman finance stories" (faceless, 5K–50K)
- "POV finance animated wealth building story" (faceless, 5K–50K)
- "economic explainer animation money explained" (faceless, 5K–50K)
- Handle fuzzy match on "povfinance"
- Outlier query: "POV finance money psychology animated story" (min breakout 5, 180 days)
- Outlier query: "stealth wealth quiet millionaire" (min breakout 4, 180 days)
- Channel-scoped outlier pulls on 15 channels across two batches

**Important tooling behaviour discovered during this pull:** `get_channel_stats` "top videos" returns a *recent-window* ranking, not a lifetime top-N. This was proven when Wealthy Logix's channel-stats top video showed 22,282 views while the outlier index held a 402,335-view video from the same channel. Consequence: channel-stats top-video lists **cannot** be used to disprove the existence of a larger lifetime video. Where an earlier figure was not re-indexed, it is marked "carried", not "disproven".

# 9. Content gaps

## 9.1 Gap 1 — Animated POV in a non-English market (corrected and narrowed)

**What the original report said:** UK, India and Australia creators in this subscriber band are "almost entirely face-cam — the animated/POV format is wide open there."

**Why that was too strong:** the fresh discovery sweep found multiple in-band faceless channels localised to those markets — British Finance with Jack (GB, 16.7K), Canadian Finance with David (CA, 15.8K), Aussie Finance With Luke (AU, 28.4K), Growth Story Studio (IN, 44.1K). The localised faceless finance space is not empty.

**What the evidence actually supports:** those localised channels are **narration-and-footage formats** — read from their titles, tags and tag discipline rather than from visual inspection, so treat the format call as inference, not verification. What is genuinely thin is **animated second-person POV in a language other than English**. Across this entire sweep, exactly one channel does it: ЦЕНА РИСКА, in Russian, 20 videos, 14.1K subs, +25.69% subs and +45.46% views in 30 days, and a 171,206-view video published seven days before this report at 24,458 views/day.

That is a much narrower claim than the original, and it is the one the data supports.

## 9.2 Gap 2 — The wealth-ladder format has no franchise owner

The device appears in at least two channels. Dark Ledger runs it repeatedly: banks $0–$25M (1,141,946 views, 237.7× breakout), net worth $0–$10M, parents $0–$1B. Finance POV runs it too: government $0–$1T, parents $0–$1B.

**What is missing:** a numbered, ongoing series with a fixed name, a fixed promise and a stable cadence. The highest-performing structural device in this niche is being used opportunistically by two channels and systematised by none. The original claim — that "no other channel has turned that structure into a repeatable numbered series" — should be softened to "no channel owns it as a franchise," because a second channel does use it.

## 11.3 Metric caveats applied throughout

- **Breakout score** is a multiple of the source baseline's mean views per video for that channel. It is a ratio, not a rate, a velocity or a forecast.
- **VPH** is views per HOUR on the source's own unspecified measurement window. It is used for relative momentum between videos only. It is never a lifetime average unless labelled `vph_all_time`, and never a live rate.
- **Views per day** on channel-stats tables is a *lifetime-average* daily rate (cumulative views ÷ days since publication). It is not current daily momentum.
- **Views per subscriber** as used here is lifetime average views per video ÷ current subscribers. It is not a subscription conversion rate.
- **Growth percentages** are 30-day index figures, not analytics. The benchmark channel is not connected to this session, so no private analytics — retention, traffic sources, impressions, CTR, revenue — are available for any channel in this report.
- **Visual format calls** for most newly discovered channels are inferred from channel descriptions and tag patterns. Only the original ten were described in the source data with explicit format language, and even there, format was not visually verified by watching the videos.
- **Null and missing metrics are unavailable, not zero.** Like counts were not returned for POV Finance's videos; that is a data gap, not a real zero. Conversely, a channel-reported zero (for example Money Made Visible's zero lifetime views) is a real measurement, not a gap.

## 11.4 What would falsify the main conclusions

- If POVFinance's +57.79% subscriber growth is a reporting artefact rather than real, the "benchmark is the growth leader" conclusion fails.
- If British Finance with Jack, Canadian Finance with David and Aussie Finance With Luke turn out to use animated POV rather than narration over footage, the localisation gap narrows to nothing and should be withdrawn.
- If POV Wealth's 337,462 views at six days turns out to be a paid-promotion spike rather than organic discovery, the "highest-velocity video in the niche" finding weakens substantially. Nothing in this data distinguishes paid from organic reach.

# 12. Metric definitions used throughout

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Views | The source's cumulative view count for a video or channel. Not views gained in a window. |
| Views per day | Lifetime-average daily rate = cumulative views ÷ days since publication. Not today's views. |
| VPH | Views per HOUR on the source's stated window. Where the window is unspecified, treated as relative momentum only. |
| VPH (all time) | Lifetime-average views per HOUR since publication. Not current velocity. |
| Breakout score | Multiple of the channel's average views per video. A ratio. |
| Engagement rate | Source-reported engagement rate for the video (likes + comments + shares relative to views, per the source's own definition). |
| Views per subscriber | Lifetime average views per video ÷ current subscriber count. Used here as a rough reach-to-audience ratio, not a conversion rate. |
| Subs / views growth (30d) | Percentage change over 30 days from vidIQ's periodically rebuilt channel index. |
| Faceless | Source-flagged as having no face on camera, or inferred from channel description for newly discovered channels. |
