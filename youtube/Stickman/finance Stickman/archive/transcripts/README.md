# 📁 transcripts/ — Master Benchmark Calibration Transcripts

## 🎯 Purpose
Permanent repository of full-length, verified competitor transcripts used to extract the channel's master Quality Gate and train/calibrate AI scriptwriting prompts.

## ✅ What is REQUIRED Here
1. **Tier S Outlier Transcripts**:
   - Complete spoken text of verified viral hits from small channels (e.g., Ollie Finance's *POV: You Quietly Built a Second Income and Told No One*, POV Finance's *Level 1*).
2. **Same-Channel Flop Transcripts**:
   - Complete spoken text of underperforming videos from the same creators (e.g., *Buying Nothing for a Year*, *Consumer Relapse*).
3. **`calibration_summary.md`**:
   - The contrastive breakdown showing why the outliers worked and why the flops failed (used in `VIDIQ_MASTER_PROMPT_WITH_TRANSCRIPTS.md`).

## ❌ What is NOT REQUIRED / FORBIDDEN
- Do NOT hoard hundreds of generic transcripts. You only need the core 4–6 benchmark pairs.
- Do NOT store uncleaned YouTube auto-generated captions with bad timing tags.
