# 📁 transcripts/ — Competitor Research & Gap Mapping

## 🎯 Purpose
Stores the scraped transcripts of top-performing videos on this specific topic, along with the Overlap/Gap Map analysis.

## ✅ What is REQUIRED Here
1. **3 to 5 Raw Competitor Transcripts**:
   - Pulled via `vidiq_video_transcript` or YouTube transcript tools for videos with $>5\times$ subscriber view counts.
   - Saved as clean text or markdown (e.g. `competitor_01_ollie.txt`).
2. **`gap_analysis.md`**:
   - **Saturated Points**: List of generic advice/tropes competitors repeated that we skip or summarize in 1 sentence.
   - **Knowledge & Emotional Gaps**: Unaddressed emotional friction, relationship fallout, or real economic mechanics found in comment heatmaps.
   - **The 40% Rule**: At least 40% of our script narrative must be built from these gaps.

## ❌ What is NOT REQUIRED / FORBIDDEN
- Do NOT hoard transcripts of general finance videos unrelated to this specific episode.
- Do NOT rewrite or copy competitor lines verbatim.
