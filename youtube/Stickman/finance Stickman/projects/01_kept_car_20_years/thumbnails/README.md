# 📁 thumbnails/ — High-CTR Packaging & Diegetic UI

## 🎯 Purpose
Hosts thumbnail concept generation, prompt iterations, and the final scored thumbnail asset adhering to the 8 Forensic Thumbnail Laws.

## ✅ What is REQUIRED Here
1. **`thumbnail_final.png`**:
   - Resolution: Exactly 1280x720, under 2 MB file size.
   - Compliance with the 8 Forensic Laws:
     1. Recurring stickman protagonist with identical visual silhouette across all videos.
     2. Protagonist shows ZERO emotion (neutral stoic anchor). Supporting character carries ALL high-arousal emotion.
     3. Contrast rendered as real-world objects, NOT words (e.g. 2005 sedan vs shiny financed SUV).
     4. POV signal carried by composition/perspective, NEVER floating "POV" text.
     5. Warm, semi-realistic suburban American environments (NO dark void-black).
     6. Numbers appear only as diegetic UI (cracked phone screen, bank statement, folded receipt).
     7. Diegetic delta integrated into surfaces (e.g. `+$780,000` on an envelope).
     8. Supporting character explicitly cast and named with specific emotion.
   - **Safe Zone**: Bottom-right corner ($180 \times 40\text{ px}$) strictly 100% free of vital elements/faces/numbers (timestamp protection).
2. **`metadata.md`**:
   - Chosen video title scored $\ge 92/100$ on vidIQ.
   - Top 15–20 tags, video description with chapters and mandatory AI disclosure confirmation.

## ❌ What is NOT REQUIRED / FORBIDDEN
- NO clickbait red arrows, glowing circles, or cartoonish graphics.
- NO floating words like "SHOCKING", "RICH VS BROKE", or "POV".
