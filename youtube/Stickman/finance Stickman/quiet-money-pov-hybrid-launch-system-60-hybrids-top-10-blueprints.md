### Why this exact order

**Episodes 1–2 buy cold reach.** Both are instantly legible to a viewer who has never heard
of your channel: everyone has owned a car too long, and everyone has slept in a cheap room.
No niche vocabulary required. Episode 1 carries the highest score returned all session (99)
and Episode 2 inherits the strongest proven breakout shape in this niche — a 3,320-sub
channel at a 4,351.8x breakout multiple.

**Episodes 3–4 raise the stakes and lock identity.** By now the viewer trusts the mechanics,
so you can hand them the emotional comparison (two identical salaries) and the belief
inversion (the house that was supposed to be the win). These convert a casual viewer into
someone who agrees with your channel's worldview.

**Episode 5 is the binge engine.** The ladder format is the strongest browse-feed structure
in this niche and you already own the wealth version. The credit-score version gives you a
second ladder axis with the same retention shape — the viewer walks up tiers hunting for
their own number.

**Episodes 6–7 are the payoffs and the debate.** Episode 6 is the accumulated outcome of
Episodes 1, 2 and 4 — the reason to still be watching. Episode 7 is the comment engine:
"max out your 401(k)" is doctrine, and questioning doctrine produces argument, which is the
strongest signal the suggestion feed reads.

**Episodes 8–9 are the inversions and the rhythm break.** Episode 8 flips the belief viewers
arrived with (that rent is dead money) — counter-intuitive framings are the highest-performing
cognitive-dissonance play available. Episode 9 changes tone entirely, so the channel does not
read as one-note preaching.

**Episode 10 is the permanent search asset.** Evergreen fee-mechanic content keeps
accumulating views long after the browse wave drops, and it is the episode that plugs into
an existing, angry, high-intent search community.

**Sequencing rule to remember:** impressions convert to *subscribers* only after a viewer has
met the channel twice. Episode 1 buys reach, Episodes 2–4 build recognition, Episodes 5–7
build the binge, Episodes 8–10 bank durable traffic.
