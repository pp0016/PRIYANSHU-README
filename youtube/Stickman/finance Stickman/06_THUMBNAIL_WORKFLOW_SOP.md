# 🖼️ SOP 06: Thumbnail Reverse-Engineering & Design (Quiet Money POV)

> **Purpose:** Engineer high-CTR POV wealth psychology thumbnails using the 8 competitive forensic laws, warm semi-realistic American environments, and diegetic UI elements to maximize Tier-1 CTR without clickbait penalties.

---

## 1. The 8 Thumbnail Laws (From Competitive Forensics)

Derived from competitive forensics across 70+ channels and 50+ outlier videos (breakout ratios up to $1,413.9\times$). Every thumbnail must adhere strictly to all eight laws:

1. **Recurring Illustrated Protagonist (Consistent Face + Topic-Adaptive Attire):** Consistent 2D cel-shaded character across every video thumbnail (brand asset, not decorative illustration). Must maintain an identical facial identity (side-swept dark brown hair, calm observant eyes, natural jawline, 100% stoic neutral expression) across all thumbnails, while wardrobe adapts diegetically to the episode theme (e.g., office shirt for corporate topics, work boots/canvas for trades, casual neutral wear for domestic topics; falling back to the signature oatmeal sweater when no specific requirement exists per `LOCKED_CHARACTER_DNA_CHEAT_SHEET.md`). The YouTube feed registers the channel face instantly at mobile scale.
2. **Protagonist Shows NO Emotion (The Stoic Anchor):** The protagonist displays a completely neutral, deadpan, unreadable expression. **The supporting cast carries ALL emotional load**—a smug boss, a wide-eyed coworker dropping their coffee, an envious relative glaring across a table, or whispering neighbors. The calm protagonist reflects financial sovereignty against external societal chaos.
3. **Contrast Rendered as OBJECTS, Not Words:** Visual contrast replaces text labels entirely. Never write *"RICH VS BROKE"*. Contrast physical real-world artifacts: an unpretentious 2005 sedan parked beside a glossy financed luxury SUV; a modest 1,100 sq ft suburban ranch home next to an ostentatious McMansion; a packed brown-bag lunch beside a $45 bistro tab; a worn kitchen ledger beside an unopened collection notice.
4. **'POV' Signal Carried by Composition, NOT Text Overlay:** Camera perspective implies first-person immersion (over-the-shoulder view, driver's perspective looking through a windshield, eye-level across an office desk, looking down at a kitchen table). **Zero "POV" text overlay.** Floating "POV" text creates banner blindness and triggers clone-channel penalties.
5. **Warm, Semi-Realistic, Culturally American Environments:** Suburban driveways, corporate cubicles, parking lots (Costco/Walmart), warm kitchen tables, coffee shop counters. **No dark void black backgrounds.** Forensics prove warm, relatable environments outperform void-black aesthetics by over $3\times$ in sustained click-through velocity across Tier-1 audiences (US, UK, CA, AU).
6. **Numbers Appear as Diegetic UI Only:** Digits must exist organically within the scene—a cracked smartphone display showing a banking app balance of `$412,890.14`, an itemized grocery receipt, a folded property tax statement, a dual-monitor brokerage portfolio. **NEVER as headline text floating in air.**
7. **Numbers on Diegetic Surfaces Only (The Integrated Asset Law):** Milestone deltas or balance amounts (e.g., `+$780,000`) must be embedded directly into physical surfaces within the scene—printed on a bank envelope, shown as an illuminated dashboard readout, or chalked on an accountant's balance sheet—integrated naturally into perspective, shadow, and lighting.
8. **Supporting Character Casting Is as Crucial as the Protagonist:** Every thumbnail brief must explicitly **NAME the supporting character** and **NAME their specific high-arousal emotional state** (e.g., *"Supporting Character: Dave (Coworker), Emotion: Envious disbelief, wide pupils, slack jaw"*). Viewers project their own social anxieties onto the supporting cast.

## 2. Color Palette & Lighting Architecture

Avoid cold sci-fi or dark void palettes. Ground the composition in warm, relatable, suburban American tones:

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Base Environment** | Warm Cream / Parchment | `#F4E9D0` | Morning sunlight, interior walls, kitchen warmth |
| **Atmospheric Fill** | Suburban Sky Blue | `#87CEEB` | Daylight, exterior driveways, office windows |
| **Grounded Base** | Suburban Lawn Green | `#4CAF50` | Landscaping, driveways, natural outdoor setting |
| **Accent Highlight** | Golden Hour Sunlight | `#FFD700` | Rim lighting on protagonist, table lamps, sunrise warmth |
| **Diegetic Asset** | Emerald Compound Green | `#10B981` | Positive bank balance readouts, investment gains UI |
| **Diegetic Conflict** | Crimson Friction Red | `#EF4444` | Past-due notices, warning lights, debt statements |
| **Line Geometry** | Deep Slate Shadow | `#1E293B` | Clean vector linework, character silhouettes |

* **The Anti-Void Rule:** Eliminate deep void black (`#050505`) backgrounds. Dark void backgrounds trigger cold crime/documentary framing, which depresses click velocity for lifestyle and wealth psychology content.
* **Safe Zone Geometry:** Reserve the bottom-right corner ($180 \times 40\text{ px}$ at $1280 \times 720\text{ px}$) 100% free of vital visual elements, faces, or diegetic numbers to prevent YouTube duration timestamp occlusion.

## 3. Four POV Thumbnail Concept Archetypes

Replace scandal/market-crash imagery with psychological wealth dynamics:

### Concept A: The Quiet Flex (Stealth Wealth / Social Incredulity)
* **Visual Composition:** Over-the-shoulder medium shot. Protagonist stands in a driveway wearing a neutral hoodie, holding ordinary car keys with a calm, neutral expression. Background shows a modest suburban home.
* **Supporting Character:** Neighbor or coworker leaning out of a brand-new $90,000 financed truck, staring at protagonist with jaw dropped in stunned disbelief.
* **Contrast Object:** Old reliable car parked in driveway vs massive monthly payment truck.
* **Diegetic UI:** Modest house keys or clean driveway title transfer slip.
* **Text:** None.

### Concept B: The Two Lives Split (Binary Life-Math / Path Divergence)
* **Visual Composition:** 50/50 vertical split comparison showing two divergent financial trajectories.
  - *Left (The Quiet Path):* Protagonist in casual attire packing lunch at a sunlit kitchen table; counter displays a smartphone showing a green `$342,000` index fund balance. Calm, relaxed posture.
  - *Right (The Status Trap):* Peer dressed in an expensive suit gripping the wheel of a luxury vehicle in traffic; dashboard screen flashes red: `$1,250/mo PAST DUE`. Visible panic and sweat beads.
* **Contrast Object:** Modest lunch prep vs luxury vehicle payment stress.
* **Text:** None. Contrast is carried 100% by objects and facial reactions.

### Concept C: The Bank Balance Reveal (Diegetic UI / Over-The-Shoulder Discovery)
* **Visual Composition:** Close-up third-person view over the protagonist's shoulder in an office breakroom or coffee shop booth.
* **Diegetic UI:** Protagonist holds a smartphone displaying an authentic mobile banking dashboard showing `$520,418.90` in liquid checking/brokerage assets.
* **Protagonist:** Unflinching, neutral, completely composed.
* **Supporting Character:** Coworker standing behind, peering over the protagonist's shoulder with wide bulging eyes, paper cup frozen mid-sip in pure shock.
* **Text:** None. The diegetic phone interface provides the curiosity gap.

### Concept D: The Social Friction (Peer Pressure / Family Dynamics)
* **Visual Composition:** Eye-level wide shot of an American dining room table or restaurant booth during a family dinner.
* **Protagonist:** Seated at the head or center of the table, calm posture, neutral face, unbothered.
* **Supporting Characters:** Surrounding family members displaying high-arousal conflicting emotions—father pointing an accusatory finger, relative glaring with arms crossed in envy, mother staring in confusion at a restaurant bill or house contract.
* **Contrast Object:** Modest worn wallet on the table next to an expensive multi-page dinner check.
* **Text:** None.

## 4. Nano Banana 2 Thumbnail Prompt Framework & Visual QA Pass

### Master Prompt Template:
```text
Warm 2D illustrated scene in semi-realistic American suburban setting. [PROTAGONIST: A calm stick figure character wearing [CONSISTENT_DESIGN_ELEMENT, e.g. dark grey minimalist hoodie] with a completely neutral expression, clean black vector lines]. [SUPPORTING CHARACTER: A [friend/family member/coworker] showing [SPECIFIC_EMOTION, e.g. envious disbelief, wide shocked eyes, open mouth]]. [ENVIRONMENT: e.g. suburban two-car driveway on a sunny morning, open-plan office breakroom, diner booth]. [CONTRAST OBJECTS: e.g. modest 2005 sedan parked adjacent to a glossy luxury SUV / smartphone displaying mobile banking interface with crisp diegetic UI]. Warm golden hour lighting, clean commercial editorial illustration style, balanced 16:9 composition, bottom-right 180x40px corner completely empty, zero text overlay, zero floating words, zero watermarks.
```

### Production Example (Concept C — Bank Balance Reveal):
```text
Warm 2D illustrated scene in semi-realistic American office breakroom setting. PROTAGONIST: A calm stick figure character wearing a minimal charcoal crewneck with a completely neutral expression, clean bold vector linework. SUPPORTING CHARACTER: A male coworker in business casual standing behind protagonist, holding a coffee mug frozen in mid-air, wide bulging eyes, jaw dropped in complete shock. ENVIRONMENT: Bright modern office breakroom with soft morning sunlight streaming through blinds, water cooler in background. CONTRAST OBJECT: Protagonist is holding an illuminated smartphone screen displaying a clean diegetic banking app interface with a green $542,000 balance clearly visible. Warm atmospheric lighting, subtle depth of field blur on background, balanced 16:9 composition, bottom-right 180x40px corner completely empty, zero floating text, zero watermarks.
```

### Mandatory Visual QA Pass (AI Artifact Elimination):
Audience comment forensics confirm viewers actively scrutinize AI generation errors. Any visual defect destroys authority. Audit every candidate thumbnail against this checklist before approval:

- [ ] **Anatomy Integrity:** Confirm hands holding phones, keys, or coffee cups have exactly 5 fingers (or clean stylized 3-finger stickman hands). Reject hand-melting or digit warping.
- [ ] **Diegetic UI Text Legibility:** Numbers on phone screens, bank statements, or receipts must be crisp, sharp, and mathematically plausible (`$542,000`, not gibberish pseudo-letters). If Nano Banana 2 distorts digits, manually composite crisp UI typography in post-production.
- [ ] **Protagonist Expression Zero-Point:** Ensure protagonist has zero smirk, smile, or distress. Must remain completely deadpan.
- [ ] **Supporting Cast Emotional Readability:** Supporting character's emotion must remain clearly legible when downscaled to $100 \times 56\text{ px}$ mobile feed size.
- [ ] **No Hallucinated Floating Typography:** Ensure no stray letterforms, faux logos, or AI watermark smears are present anywhere in the frame.

## 5. Scoring & A/B Multi-Variant Launch Strategy

### Pre-Flight Quality Scoring
* Submit generated thumbnails to the vidIQ Thumbnail Scorer (`vidiq_score_thumbnail`).
* **Approval Gate:** Must score $\ge 85/100$. Any thumbnail scoring $< 85$ is discarded and re-prompted.

### Dual-Variant Production Protocol
For every video, produce two structurally distinct concepts:
* **Variant 1 (Primary):** Direct social reaction or diegetic reveal (Concept A or Concept C).
* **Variant 2 (Challenger):** System split or social conflict (Concept B or Concept D).

### Performance Triggers & Swapping Rules
* **Target Benchmark:** **$5.0\% \text{ to } 8.0\%$ CTR** sustained over 72 hours across Tier-1 impressions.
* **24-Hour Review Gate:**
  - If $\text{Impressions} \ge 500$ and $\text{CTR} < 3.8\%$: Swap immediately to Variant 2.
  - If $\text{CTR} \ge 5.0\%$: Retain Variant 1. Archive winning prompt parameters in project notes.
* **48-Hour Review Gate:**
  - If swapped Variant 2 remains $< 3.5\%$, initiate Title re-packaging: rotate the title formula (e.g., from *POV Consequence Hook* to *Wealth Ladder* or *Binary Life-Math*) while holding Variant 2.
