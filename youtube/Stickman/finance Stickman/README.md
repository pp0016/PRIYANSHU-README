### [Director Stage] Screenplay Direction & Video Editor Blueprinting (Anti-Slop Bridge)
* **Governing File:** [`screenplay_director_manifest-part-of-4-or-screenplay-writing-.md`](file:///c:/Users/renu5/Downloads/priyanshu%20readme/youtube/Stickman/finance%20Stickman/screenplay_director_manifest-part-of-4-or-screenplay-writing-.md)
* **Why Placed Here (Crucial Pipeline Role):**
  - Generating images directly from raw script tags produces static, disconnected illustrations ("generic slop") and a naive slideshow.
  - The Director Manifest acts as the professional human video editor bridge between narration and rendering. It ingests `script_v1.md` and the millisecond timestamps from `cut_durations.json` (Step 3) to compile a machine-parseable production blueprint (`projects/[NN]_[topic]/screenplay.md`).
* **Core Capabilities & Actions:**
  1. **Topic-Adaptive Wardrobe & Consistent Face Law (First Action):** Before writing scenes, the Director designs the protagonist's wardrobe based on the specific topic's narrative setting (e.g., corporate commuter shirt, rugged trade jacket, bank meeting attire), while locking the **consistent face across all videos** (2D cel-shaded, side-swept dark brown hair, calm observant eyes, 100% neutral/peaceful face). Reverts to default oatmeal-cream sweater when no story requirement exists.
  2. **58 Hardware `xfade` Transitions:** Assigns deliberate visual transitions:
     - `zoomin` / `hblur` for high-retention hook openers (Tier 1).
     - `slideleft` / `slideright` for comparative financial arguments.
     - `wipeleft` / `wipetl` for unmasking documents, spreadsheets, and contracts.
     - `fadeblack` / `fadewhite` / `dissolve` for dramatic 4-act chapter pivots.
  3. **31 Verified Remotion Sound Effects:** Cues exact SFX assets from `assets/sfx/` (`whoosh.wav`, `vine-boom.wav`, `dramatic-boomer.wav`, `shutter-modern.wav`, `switch.wav`) normalized to -18 LUFS.
  4. **Audio Pre-Roll Offset Rule:** Automatically offsets whooshes and whips **1 to 2 frames (33ms to 60ms before the cut)** so the audio crest hits synchronously with the visual transition.
  5. **6 Void Canvas Kinetic Typography Archetypes:** For conceptual stretches and data beats without stickman illustrations, tags dynamic typography:
     - `punch` (High-impact keyword/metric on yellow/red badge).
     - `waterfall` (Spoken cadence word-pop).
     - `terminal` (JetBrains Mono memo/code decryption).
     - `odometer` (Rapid numerical ticker for dollar totals).
     - `escalator` (Stacked hierarchical steps).
     - `strikethrough` (Myth vs reality debunking).
  6. **Directs Step 4 & Step 5:** Visual asset generation (SOP 04) prompts images matching the exact screenplay camera motions and typography zones; FFmpeg assembly (SOP 05) compiles the transitions, ducked audio, and SFX with zero quality degradation (`-crf 17 -pix_fmt yuv420p`).
