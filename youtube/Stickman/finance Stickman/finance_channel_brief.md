## ⚠️ Anti-Patterns to Avoid (Proven Channel Killers)

1. **Don't put 'stickman' in the channel name** — 5 stickman finance channels found; largest has only 19 subs. The stickman is your visual production vehicle; the brand is human financial reality.
2. **Don't use 'POV Finance [word]' naming** — 16 clones exist; namespace is polluted.
3. **Don't narrow to one sub-topic** — Hudson Bay (tax only) collapsed views to hundreds. Keep scenarios broad (careers, cars, family, banking, stealth habits).
4. **Don't skip real-rate math verification** — Audience actively roasts and comments on inflated returns (100+ like callouts). Always pin to 7–8% real S&P 500 returns.
5. **Don't allow emotional faces on the hero** — The protagonist remains stoic/neutral. The supporting cast carries all drama, envy, and disbelief.
6. **Don't let the 20–25 minute runtime drag** — Never stretch a 10-minute script. Enforce the 4-Act Tension Arc with new friction introduced every 5–6 minutes.
