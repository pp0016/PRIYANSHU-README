## 🔍 2. Why the Original List Was Discarded & Re-Engineered

### The Clone Saturation Problem
The initial research identified strong keyword breakouts (e.g., *"How Banks Treat You at Every Level of Wealth — $0 to $25M"* with 1.14M views). However, forensic analysis revealed:
1. Over 16 copycat channels cloned that exact title with low-effort scripts, resulting in impressions suppression (videos stranded at 0–50 views).
2. Pure keyword-based topics without psychological dissonance attract search traffic but fail to trigger browse/suggested binging.
3. Two original candidates had confirmed zero search volume or poor demographics:
   - *Homeownership Myth* & *High Income Poor*: Recommendation-blocked.
   - *Middle Class Trap*: 66.7% volume from India (conflicts with our Tier-1 English $20–$45 RPM mandate).

### The Three Opus Selection Filters
Claude Opus re-evaluated all 64 hybrid candidates against three strict criteria:
* **Filter 1 (Pure Curiosity Gap):** A scrolling viewer at 11:00 PM cannot answer the title in their head; they must click to resolve the cognitive dissonance.
* **Filter 2 (20-Minute Narrative Stamina):** The topic must contain at least 4 natural tension resets (escalating stakes, named characters, reversals) so it doesn't run dry at minute 10.
* **Filter 3 (Subscriber Conversion):** The ending delivers an identity the viewer wants to own ("I am the kind of person who builds quietly").

### What Was Cut by Opus and Why:
* **"POV: Your $650 Car Payment Built Someone Else's $793,000"** (Moved to Day 10): Rage-click framing drives high initial views but lower subscription loyalty than the pride-framing of Day 1's *"Here's What It Built"*.
* **"How Pawn Shops Actually Make Money"** (Moved to Day 8): Search surges are snapshots; launch week must prioritize browse-feed algorithm training.
* **"How Are Americans Affording $600,000 Homes on $55,000 Salaries?"** (Reframed into Day 7): The raw question runs dry at 12 minutes ("debt and dual income"). Reframing into second-person consequence (*"POV: You Bought the $600,000 House"*) sustains 22 minutes of emotional narrative.
* **"POV: You Retired With $1.8M and Still Felt Broke"** (Deferred to Video 15+): 18–35 demographic does not feel visceral urgency around retirement yet.
* **"How Gyms Make Money From Members Who Never Show Up"** (Eliminated): The gym breakage model is common knowledge; zero information gap remaining.
