## 🎯 The Core Character Law: Consistent Face + Topic-Adaptive Wardrobe

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **IMMUTABLE DESIGN RULE:** We do **NOT** rigidly force the exact same clothes in every video.
> - **Consistent Face Across All Videos:** The protagonist's face, hair, eye calm, natural jaw contours, and completely neutral/peaceful stoic expression **NEVER CHANGE**. The face is the recognized channel asset.
> - **Topic-Adaptive Wardrobe:** Before generating a screenplay or images, **first define the character's costume based on the specific episode's narrative requirements**:
>   - *Corporate / Office / Wall Street:* Crisp commuter jacket or sky-blue oxford shirt (rolled sleeves), dark slate trousers, commuter bag.
>   - *Trades / Blue-Collar / Utility:* Rugged canvas jacket or work shirt, dark durable trousers, work boots.
>   - *Institutional / Bank Confrontation:* Understated charcoal blazer or minimal tailored collared shirt contrasting a flashy banker.
>   - *College / Young Saver / Rent:* Neutral collegiate crewneck/hoodie, dark denim.
> - **Default Embedded Fallback:** When the story has no specific career or environmental requirement, use the embedded baseline look: oatmeal-cream heavy ribbed knit crewneck sweater, dark olive tailored trousers, jade signet ring.

## 🚫 Negative Prompts & Anti-Drift Guardrails

To prevent character distortion when batch-generating 500+ images for episodes:

1. **NO "Giga Chad" Features:** Do NOT add words like *"sharp chiselled jawline, strong angular chin, masculine jaw, bodybuilder, muscular build"*. These ruin the gentle, minimal character design.
2. **NO Smug / Angry Expressions:** The hero NEVER smirks, smiles, sneers, or looks distressed. The supporting characters (coworkers, salesmen, relatives) carry ALL emotion and drama.
3. **NO Floating Text / Watermarks:** Numbers must only exist diegetically on phones, ledgers, or receipts.
4. **NO Photorealism or 3D Render:** Maintain clean black 2D outlines and flat cel-shading at all times.
