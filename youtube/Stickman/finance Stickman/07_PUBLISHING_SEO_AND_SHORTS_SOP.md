## 1. Publishing Slot & Metadata Architecture

### Publishing Window & Cadence
* **Cadence:** **Daily high-impact videos (1 video per day — 7 videos per week)**. Maximizes algorithmic momentum and library compounding, delivering over 2,600 watch hours per week.
* **Publishing Time:** **3:00 PM EST** (12:00 PM PST / 8:00 PM GMT) every single day to capture the US morning-afternoon transition and UK/European evening traffic peak.
* **Target Video Runtime:** **20 to 25 minutes** (3,800 to 4,700 words at 180–195 WPM pacing).

### Title Optimization Protocol
* Must score **$\ge 92/100$** on the vidIQ Title Scorer.
* Rotate strictly across the **6 Research-Validated Title Formulas**:
  1. **POV Consequence Hook:** `POV: You [action] — [quiet consequence]` *(e.g., "POV: You Won the Lottery — And Showed Up to Work")*
  2. **Wealth Ladder:** `How [entity] Treats You at Every Level of Wealth — $0 to $[X]` *(e.g., "How Banks Treat You at Every Level of Wealth — $0 to $10M")*
  3. **Binary Life-Math:** `You Pick [A], Your [Peer] Picks [B]: [Timeline]` *(e.g., "You Pick a Trade, Your Brother Picks College: The 20-Year Math")*
  4. **Localized Incredulity:** `How Are [Demo] Affording [Asset] on [Wage]?!` *(e.g., "How Are Americans Affording $500K Houses on $55K Salaries?")*
  5. **Threshold Inflection:** `Why Everything Changes After $[X]` *(e.g., "Why Net Worth Explodes After $100K")*
  6. **Stealth Wealth Inversion:** `[#] [Habits/Rules] That Quietly Build [Fortune/Wealth]` *(e.g., "7 Quiet Wealth Habits That Secretly Build Millions")*

### Mandatory AI Disclosure (YouTube Policy Compliance)
* Always check the **"Altered or Synthetic Content"** box under Advanced Settings in YouTube Studio.
* **Impact:** Imposes zero reach penalty while granting channel immunity against automated spam takedowns and monetization review rejections.

## 2. Shorts-to-Longform Funnel Syndication Workflow

Deploy **2 to 3 vertical Shorts (9:16)** staggered across **2 to 4 hours post-upload**. In the POV wealth niche, Shorts are not isolated content—they exist strictly as a **top-of-funnel acquisition engine** driving traffic to the long-form anchor.

### Funnel Mechanics
1. **Native Related Video Link:** In YouTube Studio, set the **"Related video"** field on each Short to point directly to the published long-form video.
2. **Pinned Conversion Comment:** Pin a comment immediately upon Short publication:
   `👉 Watch the full story and complete math breakdown on my channel 👆`

### The 3 Conversion Shorts Templates

#### Short 1: The Hook Teaser (40–50s)
* **Structure:** The opening 45 seconds of the long-form video. Introduces the protagonist, the social pressure, and the quiet decision.
* **Cutoff Moment:** Cuts abruptly at the primary cliffhanger before the resolution.
* **Audio Voiceover Outro:** *"What happened next changed everything. Full story on my channel."*

#### Short 2: The Social Moment (30–40s)
* **Structure:** Highlights the most emotionally charged social friction scene from the script (e.g., the confrontation at the family dinner table or coworker discovering the bank balance).
* **Narrative Focus:** Focuses on human emotional tension (disbelief, envy, judgment) rather than numbers.
* **CTA:** On-screen text badge: *"See how it played out ➡️ Full video linked below."*

#### Short 3: The Number Reveal (25–35s)
* **Structure:** Fast-paced presentation of the 10-year financial inflection point. Shows the diegetic balance on-screen with the realistic compound interest divergence.
* **Ending:** Halts on the striking visual delta between the two paths.
* **CTA:** *"Full calculation and story linked below."*

## 3. Community Tab Engagement Strategy

POV channels achieve massive impression reach but often suffer low community depth. Channels like *Pigeon Finance* achieve **5.88% engagement rates** by running high-resonance dilemma polls. Execute **1 to 2 community polls per week** to prime the algorithmic engagement graph between long-form uploads.

### Schedule
* **Poll 1:** Wednesday 12:00 PM EST (Mid-week engagement booster)
* **Poll 2:** Sunday 4:00 PM EST (Pre-week audience priming)

### High-Engagement Poll Formats

#### Dilemma A: The Social Friction Scenario
```text
You have $50,000 saved in a boring index fund. Your coworker just leased a brand new $75,000 BMW on credit and shows it off in the parking lot. 

What do you do?
[ ] (A) Say nothing and smile
[ ] (B) Break down the lease math for them
[ ] (C) Feel tempted to buy something nice too
[ ] (D) Quietly check your portfolio balance
```

#### Dilemma B: The Stealth Wealth Boundary
```text
You wake up tomorrow and your net worth quietly crosses $1,000,000 for the first time. 

Who is the first person you tell?
[ ] (A) My parents / family
[ ] (B) My partner only
[ ] (C) My closest friend
[ ] (D) Absolutely nobody on earth
```

#### Dilemma C: The Lifestyle Split
```text
Would you rather:
[ ] (A) Make $70K, spend $35K, drive an old Honda, owe $0
[ ] (B) Make $200K, spend $195K, drive a new Porsche, owe $600K
```

* **Conversion Pin:** In the top comment of every community poll, link the relevant long-form video:
  *`"We broke down the exact math behind this scenario in yesterday's video: [LINK]"`*
