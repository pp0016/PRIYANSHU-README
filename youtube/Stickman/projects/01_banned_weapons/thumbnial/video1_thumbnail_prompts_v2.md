## Phase 1: Title-Thumbnail Pairing

**Selected Pattern: Status + Context**
- Thumbnail shows WHAT (the banned weapons / the forbidden status)
- Title adds the WHY (terrifying + banned)

**Anti-redundancy check:** Title says "Terrifying" + "Banned". Thumbnail visuals must communicate "forbidden / classified / dangerous" WITHOUT using those same words. Text overlay should be ONE word max: "BANNED", "CLASSIFIED", or "FORBIDDEN" — never repeat the title.

---

## Phase 2: Design Thinking (Applied to All 6 Concepts)

### Curiosity Gap
Show weapons that LOOK devastating or LOOK classified — but don't explain WHY they're banned. The viewer must click to learn.

### Four C's
- **Clarity:** One focal point visible at phone-thumb size
- **Contrast:** Dark background + bright red/amber accent (military niche default)
- **Consistency:** Dark, military, serious tone — matches Serious History / ExplainTory visual DNA
- **Character:** Weapons ARE the characters (faceless channel). They must feel dangerous, not like stock photos.

### Skeptic / Expert / Scroller
- **Skeptic:** "Why should I click?" → Because something powerful is marked FORBIDDEN — what makes it so dangerous?
- **Expert:** Safe zone clear? Text readable at 150px? ✅
- **Scroller:** At thumb-tip size, can I tell this is about dangerous banned weapons? If yes, it passes.

### Concept 1: "The Classified Evidence Board" (Corkboard)

**Pairing Pattern:** Status + Context
**Style:** Mystery / Evidence
**Curiosity Gap:** This looks like a CRIME INVESTIGATION — what did these weapons DO to get classified?
**Emotion:** Rank 2 (Curiosity) — detective/investigation aesthetic creates "I need to solve this"
**Composition:** Z-Pattern — eye travels: red string (top-left) → weapon photos → "CLASSIFIED" stamp → evidence tags

**Visual Description:**
A dark wooden corkboard covered in pinned polaroid-style photos of infamous banned weapons, connected by red string in a web pattern. 5-6 weapon photos pinned at angles with red pushpins. A massive faded red "CLASSIFIED" rubber stamp slams diagonally across the center of the board. Dim warm side-lighting creates shadows behind the pinned photos. The overall feel is military intelligence investigation — dark, urgent, secretive.

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Dark cork/brown (#2a1a0a) — investigation board, grounded and serious
- Secondary: Off-white (#f5f0e0) — polaroid photo borders
- Accent: Blood red (#c41e3a) — string, pushpins, CLASSIFIED stamp

**Text Overlay:** "CLASSIFIED" — integrated INTO the image as a rubber stamp, faded red, angled diagonally. Not separate text. This IS the visual hook.

**Safe Zone:** Bottom-right has one weapon photo pinned at an angle — no critical text or details there.

**Why This Concept Works:** UNIQUE in the weapons/military niche. Nobody uses the evidence-board aesthetic. It reframes "weapons explained" as "weapons INVESTIGATED" — turning the viewer into a detective. The red string + polaroids create multiple focal points across the frame (Z-pattern), and the "CLASSIFIED" stamp adds forbidden-knowledge tension. True-crime is one of the highest-CTR aesthetics on YouTube — this borrows that energy for military content.
