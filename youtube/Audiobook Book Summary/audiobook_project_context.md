# Project Context: AI Audiobook Summary Channel

**Purpose of this document:** This is a forensic export of the project's foundational details and strategic decisions. It can be provided to any AI agent to instantly bring them up to speed on the channel's parameters without having to repeat previous conversations.

---

## 📌 Core Project Details

- **Niche:** YouTube Audiobook Summaries
- **Language Strategy:** Testing both Hindi and English simultaneously to determine which grows faster and yields better results.
- **Production Model:** ~95% AI-generated content.
    - **AI handles:** Voiceovers (TTS), visuals/images, and script summarization.
    - **Human handles:** Final quality assurance (QA) and manual checks.
- **Monetization Strategy:** 
    1. YouTube AdSense
    2. Affiliate Marketing (e.g., links to the books)
    3. Brand Sponsorships
    4. *Future Expansion:* Distributing the audio tracks on Spotify.

---

## 🔍 Strategic Decisions (The Grill Session)

*The following Q&A outlines the specific boundaries and creative decisions established during the initial planning phase.*

**Q1: What kind of books will you be summarizing? Copyrighted bestsellers (carry strike risk) or public domain classics (safer)?**
> **Decision:** A mix of both. The primary metric is performance—I will summarize whatever gets views.

**Q2: How long will your summary videos be? (Affects watch time, RPM, and ad placement)**
> **Decision:** Long-form. Minimum 45 minutes, with a maximum length of up to 3 hours. 

**Q3: Which AI voice tool are you planning to use? (Crucial for retention on 3-hour videos)**
> **Decision:** Flexible, as long as it produces high quality. Options include ElevenLabs, Google TTS, Edge TTS, or any other tool that delivers a premium listening experience.

**Q4: What will the visual style of your videos be?**
> **Decision:** Minimal and standard for the audiobook niche. The visual focus is low-priority because the audience primarily listens. The format will likely feature the book cover/thumbnail on one side and captions/text on the right side (similar to a Spotify podcast layout).
