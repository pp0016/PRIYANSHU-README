TOPIC 10 (Tier 3, asked 1/9): ENERGY PRICING

Q: Explain in brief energy pricing. (3 marks)

Energy pricing is the mechanism by which energy costs are determined for consumers. In India, energy prices are set through a mix of market forces and government regulation.
- Electricity tariffs are set by State Electricity Regulatory Commissions (SERCs) and vary by consumer category (domestic, commercial, industrial, agricultural)
- Petroleum product prices (petrol, diesel) were deregulated in 2010-2014 and now follow international crude oil prices
- Natural gas prices are set by the government every 6 months using a formula linked to international gas prices
- Coal prices are set by Coal India Ltd with government oversight
- Cross-subsidization: industrial and commercial consumers pay higher rates to subsidize domestic and agricultural consumers
- Energy pricing directly affects conservation behavior. Higher prices encourage energy saving.
