### 🤖 Antigravity
Priyanshu,

**ANSWER**  
An AI can read and understand raw, unstructured text and raw PDFs perfectly fine without you restructuring them. However, converting a document to structured Markdown (MD) is objectively better for accuracy and complex reasoning.

**WHY**  
LLMs do not have human eyes; they read sequences of text. A PDF is designed for visual printing, not text extraction. When you feed an AI a raw PDF, the extracted text often breaks—multi-column layouts bleed into each other, tables turn into random strings of numbers, and paragraphs split in half. The AI has to waste its processing power just trying to piece broken sentences back together.

Markdown fixes this. It uses explicitly defined structures (like `#` for headers and `|` for tables) that exactly match how AI models were trained to process data (since we are trained heavily on code and internet markdown). When a document is in MD format, the AI spends 100% of its processing power analyzing your actual problem instead of decrypting the messy formatting.

**COUNTER**  
Converting a PDF to Markdown is slow, prone to errors during the conversion process itself, and consumes significantly more tokens. If you upload a 300-page manual just to ask, "Does this book mention the warranty policy?", forcing an MD conversion is a massive waste of time and compute. Raw text extraction handles that instantly.

**CONDITIONS**  
The "best" method depends entirely on what you are trying to do:
1. **Raw PDF / Messy Text is best for:** Quick summaries, keyword lookups, or basic Q&A where structural precision doesn't matter. It is fast and cheap.
2. **Markdown Conversion is best for:** Extracting precise data from tables, writing code based on the document's rules, or executing complex research tasks where missing a single detail causes a failure.
