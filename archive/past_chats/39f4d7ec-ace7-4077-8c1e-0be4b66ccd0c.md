## SECTION 1 — Tier 1 Audience Targeting (Indian Creator Problem)

### What Actually Moves the Needle (Ranked)

**#1 — Content Topic & Niche (Most Important)**

The topic determines your audience geography more than anything else. YouTube's algorithm is **viewer-centric, not location-centric.** When you upload, it tests your video on a small "seed" audience. If your initial subscribers are Indian and they skip your US-targeted content — the video dies. You never reach the US.

| Topics That Pull Tier 1 Audiences | Topics That Lock You Into India |
|---|---|
| Global tech/AI explainers | Hindi entertainment or drama |
| Finance (US market focus) | Indian exam prep (JEE, UPSC) |
| Science/education (universal) | Regional language content |
| True crime (global stories) | India-specific news |

Source: [Hootsuite YouTube Algorithm Guide](https://blog.hootsuite.com/how-the-youtube-algorithm-works/), [Reddit r/PartneredYoutube](https://www.reddit.com/r/PartneredYoutube/)

**#2 — Language & Accent (Critical for Retention)**

Standard English with a neutral accent is mandatory. Indian-accented English causes measurable drop-offs in US/UK retention. Solution: Use **ElevenLabs with an American or British voice** — which you already plan for Stikaman. This is the right call.
