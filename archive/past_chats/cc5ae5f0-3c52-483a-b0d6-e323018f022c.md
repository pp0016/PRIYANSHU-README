*   Roll speed ($N$) = 8 rpm
*   Moment arm coefficient ($\lambda$) = 0.5
*   Average flow stress ($\bar{Y}_f$) = 250 MPa = 250 N/mm²

**Step 1: Calculate Draft ($\Delta h$) [1 Mark]**
$$\Delta h = h_0 - h_1 = 20 - 15 = 5 \text{ mm}$$

**Step 2: Calculate Projected Length of Contact ($L_c$) [2 Marks]**
$$L_c = \sqrt{R \cdot \Delta h}$$
$$L_c = \sqrt{125 \times 5} = \sqrt{625} = 25 \text{ mm}$$
*(Note: GTU loves perfect squares. Seeing $\sqrt{625}$ confirms you are on the right track).*

**Step 3: Calculate Roll Force ($F$) [1.5 Marks]**
$$F = \bar{Y}_f \times w \times L_c$$
$$F = 250 \text{ N/mm}^2 \times 100 \text{ mm} \times 25 \text{ mm}$$
$$F = 625,000 \text{ N} \text{ (or } 625 \text{ kN)}$$

**Step 4: Calculate Torque per roll ($T$) [1.5 Marks]**
Moment arm ($a$) = $\lambda \times L_c = 0.5 \times 25 = 12.5 \text{ mm} = 0.0125 \text{ m}$
$$T = F \times a$$
$$T = 625,000 \text{ N} \times 0.0125 \text{ m}$$
$$T = 7,812.5 \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$$

**Step 5: Calculate Total Power ($P$) [1 Mark]**
Power for TWO rolls:

### What the Diagram Is
It is a 2D cross-sectional view of a sand mould. It maps the exact "plumbing" system the liquid metal travels through. It starts at the top where the metal is poured, flows down vertically, moves horizontally, enters the cavity, and pushes excess metal up into a reservoir.
