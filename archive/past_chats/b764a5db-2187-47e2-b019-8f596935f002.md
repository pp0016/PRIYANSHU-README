Priyanshu, Final Cut Pro is the exact opposite of Premiere Pro. It is specifically designed to NOT look like an airplane cockpit.

Here is how they compare:

**1. A Clean, Hidden Interface:**
Premiere Pro puts every single tool, panel, and button on the screen at the same time, which is why it feels overwhelming. Final Cut Pro hides the advanced tools until you actually need them. When you open it, it looks very clean and minimalist—almost like a professional version of iMovie. There are far fewer visible buttons.

**2. The Magnetic Timeline (No Tracks):**
In Premiere, you edit on rigid "tracks" (Video 1, Video 2, Audio 1). If you delete a clip in the middle of your video, it leaves a black gap that you have to manually close. Final Cut uses a "Magnetic Timeline." If you delete a clip, the rest of the video automatically snaps together like magnets to fill the gap.
