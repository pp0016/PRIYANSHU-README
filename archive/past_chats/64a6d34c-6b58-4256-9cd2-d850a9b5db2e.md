## Part 1: Your Missing Constraints — Before & After

### Missing Constraint #1: No Scope Lock

**What you wrote:**
> "You have to create whole new, better skills by understanding the intent of these skills by reading them."

**The problem:** "Better" has no definition. Better how? Shorter? More accurate? Faster to trigger? The agent picks its own definition of "better" and optimizes for something you might not care about.

**How to constrain it:**
> "Read prompt-forge/SKILL.md. Create a new skill file called prompt-forge-v2/SKILL.md. The new skill must: (1) be under 200 lines, (2) keep the CREATE and ENHANCE modes, (3) add a new GAP ANALYSIS output mode. Do not modify the original file. **The rule:** Every task needs a **boundary box** — what files to touch, what NOT to touch, and a measurable definition of the output.
