TOPIC 8 (Tier 3, never asked but in new syllabus): PERFORM ACHIEVE AND TRADE (PAT) SCHEME

Q: Explain the Perform Achieve and Trade (PAT) scheme. (3-4 marks)

PAT is a market-based energy efficiency trading mechanism under the National Mission for Enhanced Energy Efficiency (NMEEE), which is one of the eight missions under India's National Action Plan on Climate Change.

How PAT works:
1. BEE identifies Designated Consumers (DCs) in energy-intensive sectors: thermal power plants, iron and steel, cement, aluminum, fertilizers, textile, pulp and paper, chlor-alkali, railways, refineries, petrochemicals, distribution companies, buildings
2. Each DC is given a Specific Energy Consumption (SEC) reduction target based on their current performance
3. DCs that reduce their SEC beyond the target earn Energy Saving Certificates (ESCerts)
4. DCs that fail to meet their target must buy ESCerts from the market or pay a penalty
5. ESCerts are traded on two power exchanges: Indian Energy Exchange (IEX) and Power Exchange India Limited (PXIL)

Results: PAT Cycle I (2012-2015) covered 478 designated consumers and achieved energy savings of 8.67 million tonnes of oil equivalent (MTOE) against a target of 6.686 MTOE. This exceeded the target by about 30%.

PAT is currently in its 7th cycle, covering over 1000 designated consumers across 13 sectors.
