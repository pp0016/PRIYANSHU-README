**BLUNT ANSWER: NO.** 
A graphics card does absolutely *nothing* to help you manage 10 Chrome profiles, 100 tabs, or multiple heavy IDEs (Antigravity/VS Code). All of that workload depends **100% on your RAM and your CPU.** 

### 3. Why is a graphics card required then? (In short)
A graphics card is required for processing pixels and parallel math. It is needed for:
*   Playing heavy 3D games.
*   Hardware-accelerated video rendering (exporting videos faster).
*   Running AI/Machine Learning models.

### 4. You use FFmpeg, Remotion, and want to run Local Open-Source AI Models. What is required?
**BLUNT ANSWER: You MUST buy a laptop with a Dedicated GPU (Nvidia RTX), NOT an integrated one.**

*   **Local Open-Source Models (LLMs):** You **cannot** run decent local AI models smoothly on integrated graphics. AI models require **VRAM** (Video RAM found on dedicated Nvidia GPUs). Without an Nvidia RTX card or an Apple Silicon Mac (M2/M3 with high unified memory), local AI models will either crash or run incredibly slow.
