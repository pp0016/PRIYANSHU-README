### 🤖 Antigravity
Running a heavily modded GTA 5 (especially with script-heavy mods like Menyoo, AddonPeds, and 10+ active custom ragdoll physics) while simultaneously recording via OBS is an intense workload. 

GTA 5 is an older engine, and it is notoriously **CPU-bound**. When you add custom ragdolls and scripts, you hammer the CPU. Add high-resolution graphic mods (like NVE or QuantV), and your GPU and VRAM take a massive hit. On a laptop, this extreme dual-load (Game + OBS) leads directly to heat, which causes **thermal throttling** (the laptop intentionally slowing itself down to prevent melting), resulting in massive lag spikes and crashes.

To avoid this, you must buy a laptop with excellent cooling and the right component balance. Always prioritize an **NVIDIA GPU** because you can use their **NVENC encoder** in OBS, which forces the GPU to handle the video recording, saving your CPU from completely choking.

Here are the exact hardware requirements to pull this off without crashing.

### 1. The Exact MINIMUM Specs
*This is the absolute floor to run these mods and record at 1080p 60fps. Expect some frame drops when multiple ragdolls interact or explosions happen.*
*   **CPU:** Intel Core i5-12500H or AMD Ryzen 5 6600H (You need at least 6 cores/12 threads. Weaker CPUs will freeze the game when physics calculations spike).
*   **GPU:** NVIDIA RTX 3060 or RTX 4050 **(6GB VRAM minimum)**. 6GB is the bare minimum so your graphic mods don't cause an "Out of Memory" crash.
*   **RAM:** 16GB DDR4 or DDR5.
*   **Storage:** 1TB NVMe SSD. (Do not get 512GB; a modded GTA 5 install can easily exceed 200GB alone).

### 4. Crucial Tips for Your Setup
1.  **Use NVENC in OBS:** When you set up OBS, go to Output settings and ensure your Encoder is set to **NVIDIA NVENC H.264** or **HEVC**. If you leave it on x264 (CPU encoding), your game *will* stutter and crash.
2.  **Elevate the Laptop:** Never play flat on a desk. Buy a cheap laptop stand to lift the back up by 2 inches so the intake fans can breathe. For heavy modding, a forced-air cooling pad (like the *IETS GT500* or *GT600*) will drop your temperatures by 10-15°C and completely prevent thermal throttling.
3.  **Lower Population Density:** In GTA 5's graphics settings, turn down "Population Density" and "Population Variety." You are adding 10+ custom ragdolls; you need to remove the random AI citizens to free up CPU cycles so the game engine doesn't choke.
