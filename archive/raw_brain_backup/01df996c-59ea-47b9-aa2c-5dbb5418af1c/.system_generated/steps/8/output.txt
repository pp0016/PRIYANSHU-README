Gabb and Pinwheel are two separate companies that offer distinct "kid-safe" smartphone ecosystems[1][2][3]. Both provide modified Android devices with parental controls, but they differ significantly in their approach to safety, app accessibility, and management[2][4].

### **Gabb Wireless (2026)**
Gabb focuses on a "minimalist" approach, emphasizing a locked-down environment with no internet browser, no app store, and no social media[5][1][2].

*   **Current Models:**
    *   **Gabb Phone 4:** Designed for ages 9–13, this is the primary entry-level model[1][6].
    *   **Gabb Phone 4 Pro:** A more advanced model designed for ages 14–18, offering more flexibility and additional apps while maintaining safety protocols[1][7][6].
*   **Key Features:** Gabb uses a custom operating system (GabbOS) to restrict device functions[1]. It includes essential tools like curated maps, music, and basic communication apps, but users cannot install third-party apps[1][8].

### **Pinwheel (2026)**
Pinwheel provides a "gradual-release" model, designed to grow with the child by allowing parents to curate a list of approved apps from a vetted library[9][10][2].

*   **Current Models:**
    *   **Genesis 4:** A popular starter smartphone[11][12].
    *   **Slim 6:** A slim, stylish model[13][12].
    *   **Plus 6:** A premium model with 5G capability and advanced hardware[12].
    *   **Pinwheel Home:** A Wi-Fi-connected, voice-only "landline" device for younger children[14].
*   **Key Features:** Pinwheel devices use a modified Android OS that allows parents to manage contacts, app access, and usage schedules through a Caregiver Portal[15][10]. Parents can choose from over 1,000 vetted apps (such as Spotify or educational tools) to enable as their child matures[10][12][2].

### **Bark Phone**
While often mentioned alongside these brands, **Bark** is a distinct service that typically monitors existing phones or provides its own managed device with robust AI-based content monitoring rather than a "closed" app ecosystem[11][2][16].

| **Carrier** | Uses Gabb Wireless service[1] | Compatible with most major carriers[12] |
| **Best For** | Parents wanting a strictly locked-down device[2] | Parents wanting to gradually introduce app functionality[9][2] |
