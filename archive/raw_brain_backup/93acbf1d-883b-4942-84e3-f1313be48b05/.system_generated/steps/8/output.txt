To transfer files between two Mac computers using Share Disk (specifically when one Mac is in macOS Recovery on Apple silicon), follow these steps[1]:

### Prerequisites
*   **Hardware:** A USB, USB-C, or Thunderbolt cable to connect the two computers[1].
*   **Status:** The Mac you wish to share from must be started up in macOS Recovery[1].

### Steps to Share and Transfer Files
1.  **Connect the Macs:** Connect the two computers using a compatible USB, USB-C, or Thunderbolt cable[1].
2.  **Start Share Disk:**
    *   On the Mac you want to share from (in macOS Recovery), choose **Utilities > Share Disk**[1][2].
    *   Select the volume you wish to share and click **Start Sharing**[1].
3.  **Access the Disk on the Other Mac:**
    *   On the other Mac, open a **Finder** window[1].
    *   Click **Network** in the sidebar under "Locations"[1].
    *   Double-click the Mac that has the shared disk[3].
    *   Click **Connect As**, select **Guest**, and then click **Connect**[3].
4.  **Transfer and Finish:**
    *   Transfer your files as needed[3].
    *   Once finished, eject the volume on the second Mac[3].
    *   On the Mac in Recovery, click **Stop Sharing** in the Share Disk app, then choose **Share Disk > Quit Share Disk**[3].
