The search for "site:support.apple.com "Universal Control: Use a single keyboard and mouse between Mac and iPad" OR "Universal Control"" returned the following summary:
Universal Control allows you to use a single keyboard, mouse, or trackpad to control up to two other nearby Mac or iPad devices[1][2]. You can move the pointer, type, and copy content seamlessly between these devices[1][3].

### **Key Features**
*   **Seamless Interaction:** Each device maintains its own screen and apps, but you can control them as if they were a single system[1].
*   **Copy and Paste:** You can copy content from one device and paste it into another[1][3].
*   **Comparison to Other Features:**
    *   **Sidecar:** Use your iPad to extend or mirror your Mac’s desktop[1][2].
    *   **iPhone Mirroring:** Use your Mac to interact with your iPhone[1].
    *   **Mac Virtual Display:** Use your Mac keyboard and trackpad to work on your Mac within Apple Vision Pro[1].

### **How to Set It Up**
1.  **System Requirements:** Ensure all devices are signed in with the same Apple Account, have Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned on, and that Handoff is enabled[1][2].
2.  **Enable on Mac:**
    *   Go to **System Settings** (or System Preferences) > **Displays**[1].
    *   Click **Advanced** and turn on **"Allow your pointer and keyboard to move between any nearby Mac or iPad"**[1].
3.  **Enable on iPad:**
    *   Go to **Settings** > **General** > **AirPlay & Continuity** (or AirPlay & Handoff)[1].
    *   Turn on **Cursor and Keyboard**[1].
4.  **Connect:** Place devices near each other (awake and unlocked) and move your pointer past the edge of the screen toward the other device to link them[1][4].
