To set up your Mac to act as an AirPlay receiver, allowing you to stream content from an iPhone, iPad, or another Mac, follow these steps based on your macOS version:

### **For macOS Ventura 13 or later**
1.  Open the **Apple menu ()** and select **System Settings**[1].
2.  Click **General** in the sidebar[2][1].
3.  Click **AirDrop & Continuity** on the right[1].
4.  Turn on **AirPlay Receiver**[2][1].
5.  Choose who can use AirPlay to stream to your Mac using the "Allow AirPlay for" pop-up menu[2]:
    *   **Current User:** Only devices signed in with the same Apple Account[2].
    *   **Anyone on the Same Network:** Allows others on your Wi-Fi[2][3].
    *   **Everyone:** Allows broader access[2][3].
6.  You can also set a password if you wish to require one for connections[2][3].

### **For macOS Monterey 12**
1.  Open the **Apple menu ()** and select **System Preferences**[1][3].
2.  Click **Sharing**[1][3].
3.  Select **AirPlay Receiver** in the service list on the left and ensure the checkbox is selected[1][3].
4.  Configure your preferred settings for "Allow AirPlay for" and set a password if needed[3].

### **Requirements**
*   **Connectivity:** Both devices must have Wi-Fi turned on and be connected to the same network[2].
*   **Compatibility:** Ensure your Mac model and macOS version support AirPlay to Mac[1]. Generally, this includes iMac Pro, 2020 or later Mac mini, and most other Mac models introduced in 2018 or later[1].
