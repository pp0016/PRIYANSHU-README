The search for ""Logic Node" Apple deprecated OR removed Logic Pro" returned the following summary:
Logic Node, the feature that allowed users to distribute audio processing across multiple networked Mac computers, has been **discontinued**[1][2].

It was effectively removed from Logic Pro when the application moved past the era of Logic Pro 9[2]. The technology is no longer a part of the modern Logic Pro workflow[1][2].

### Important Distinction
Please be careful not to confuse the discontinued "Logic Node" (the networking feature) with **automation nodes**[3]. Automation nodes are the points you place on automation curves to adjust volume, pan, or effects parameters over time[3]; these remain a core, active part of Logic Pro and are frequently discussed in user forums[4].
