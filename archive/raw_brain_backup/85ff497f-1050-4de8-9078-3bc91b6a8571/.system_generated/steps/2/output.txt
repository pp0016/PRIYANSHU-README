To access the Apple Education discount in India, you must verify your student or faculty status through **UNiDAYS**[1][2]. Apple uses this platform to confirm eligibility for their education pricing and seasonal offers like "Back to School"[1][3].

### How to Verify Your Student Status
You can typically start the verification process directly through the **Apple Education Store** website[4][2].

1.  **Visit the Apple Education Store:** Go to the official [Apple India Education Store](https://www.apple.com/in/edu/store)[5][2].
2.  **Locate the Verification Link:** Look for the option to "Verify with UNiDAYS" during the checkout process or on the Education Store landing page[6][7][4].
3.  **Sign In or Create an Account:** If you don’t have a UNiDAYS account, you will need to create one using your personal information[1][8].
4.  **Verification Methods:**
    *   **Institution Email:** The fastest way is often to use your university-provided email address (e.g., ending in `.edu` or similar)[9][10][11].
    *   **Student ID Card:** If you do not have a university email address, you can choose the option to **upload a photo of your student ID card**[10][12]. 
        *   Ensure the image is clear and shows your full name, the institution's name, and is valid for the current academic year[9].

### Important Tips
*   **Processing Time:** While many verifications are instant, it may occasionally take up to 48 hours for UNiDAYS to review your uploaded documents[9].
*   **Eligibility:** This discount is generally available to current and newly accepted university students, as well as teachers and staff at all levels of education[6][2].
*   **Documentation:** If you are a teacher or staff member, you may be asked to provide a teaching certificate or official staff ID instead of a student ID[2].
*   **Technical Issues:** If you encounter errors, ensure you are on the correct regional website (India) and that your documents meet the requirements (legible, valid dates, and not altered)[9][6].
