The Apple "Back to School" promotion in India for 2026, known as the **College Student Offer**, ran from **July 16, 2026, to August 27, 2026** (with terms referencing a broader promotion period ending September 24, 2026, for specific order fulfillments)[1][2][3].

Below are the key terms and conditions regarding the offer:

### **Offer Overview**
*   **The Benefit:** Qualified purchasers who bought an eligible Mac or iPad during the promotion period received "Promotion Savings" equal to the value of the promotional product[1][3]. 
*   **How it Worked:** While often marketed as "free" AirPods or Apple Pencil, the promotion functioned as a discount applied to the total price of the bundle[2]. You were charged for all items in your basket, but the price of the Mac or iPad was reduced by the value of the promotional product (e.g., ₹14,900 for AirPods)[1][2][3].

### **Eligibility**
To qualify for the Apple Store for Education pricing and the Back to School promotion, you must be a member of one of the following groups[4][5]:
*   **Students:** Individuals attending or accepted into a tertiary education institution in India[4].
*   **Parents:** Parents purchasing on behalf of their child who is a student at a public or private tertiary institution in India[4].
*   **Educators/Staff:** Employees of any public or private education institution in India[4][5].

### **Key Terms & Conditions**
*   **Verification:** Eligibility is typically verified through **UNiDAYS**[6][7]. You may be required to provide valid student/employee ID cards or official documentation as proof of status[8].
*   **Qualifying Locations:** The offer was valid at Apple Store locations, the Apple Store for Education (online), and via the official Apple contact centre (000800 040 1966)[1][3].
*   **Purchase Quantity:** Products purchased via the Education store are for personal, non-commercial use and are not for resale[4][8]. There are limits on the number of devices you can purchase under these discounts within a 12-month period[8].
*   **Audit Rights:** Apple reserves the right to verify your status[8]. If it is discovered that a purchaser did not meet the eligibility requirements, Apple may charge the payment method on file for the difference between the education price and the standard retail price[9].
*   **Returns:** If you return your eligible Mac or iPad, you must also return the promotional product; otherwise, the value of the promotion may be deducted from your refund[1][9].

For specific details regarding your transaction or if you require further assistance, you can contact Apple Support or visit the official [Apple India Education](https://www.apple.com/in-edu/shop) website[4][5].
