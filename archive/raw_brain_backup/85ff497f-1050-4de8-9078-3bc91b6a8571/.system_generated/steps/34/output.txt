In 2026, the **SBI Student Plus Advantage Credit Card** remains a specialized credit card offering designed for students[1][2]. It is primarily available to students who have availed of an education loan from the State Bank of India (SBI) or hold a fixed deposit (FD) with the bank[3][4][5].

### **SBI Student Plus Advantage Credit Card Overview**
*   **Eligibility:** You must typically be an SBI education loan customer or maintain a fixed deposit (minimum amount often around ₹5,000) with the bank[3][4][5]. The applicant must be at least 18 years old[3][6].
*   **Key Features:**
    *   **Rewards:** Generally offers 1 reward point for every ₹100 spent[2][4].
    *   **Fuel Waiver:** Often includes a 2.5% fuel surcharge waiver[2][6].
    *   **Fees:** Many sources indicate it as having nil joining/annual fees, with potential renewal fees (e.g., ₹500) if annual spending thresholds are not met[7][6].
*   **Application:** Applications can be processed through the official SBI Card website or by visiting an SBI branch[3].

### **How to Apply for a Student Credit Card**
1.  **Choose the Right Card:** Evaluate cards based on annual fees, reward structures, and the required FD amount[12].
2.  **Fixed Deposit Route:** Most student cards require you to open a fixed deposit[8]. Your credit limit is usually a percentage (typically 80–90%) of this deposit[4][5][11].
3.  **Documentation:** You will generally need:
    *   PAN Card[3][8]
    *   Aadhaar Card[3][8]
    *   College ID / Bonafide Certificate[3][11]
    *   FD details (if applying for a secured card)[3][9]
4.  **Online Application:** Most major banks provide an online portal or mobile app (like YONO SBI or bank-specific credit card pages) to track and submit your application[16][17][18].
