At the Apple India Education Store, pricing for the MacBook Pro series (featuring the M5 family of chips) is as follows:

### **MacBook Pro Pricing (Education Store)**
*   **14-inch MacBook Pro:**
    *   **M5 Chip:** Starting from **₹2,27,900** (or from ₹11,158/mo with EMI)[1][2].
    *   **M5 Pro Chip:** Starting from **₹2,81,900** (or from ₹13,801/mo with EMI)[3][1].
    *   **M5 Max Chip:** Starting from **₹4,62,900** (or from ₹22,663/mo with EMI)[3][1].
*   **16-inch MacBook Pro:**
    *   Starting from **₹3,35,900** (or from ₹16,445/mo with EMI)[4].

### **Additional Education Store Benefits**
*   **AppleCare+ Savings:** Eligible students and educators can save up to 10% on AppleCare+ protection[5][6].
*   **Seasonal Offers:** During promotional periods (such as "Back to School"), students may receive additional incentives, such as AirPods, with the purchase of an eligible Mac[7].
*   **Software:** Eligible Macs come with a free three-month trial of Apple Creator Studio, and students can choose to pre-install the Pro Apps Bundle for Education at a special price[3][8].

**Eligibility:** Apple Education pricing is available to current university/college students, newly accepted students, parents buying for students, and educators or staff at any educational institution[9]. Verification may be requested, so it is recommended to have your student or faculty ID ready[9].
