### Writing Formula
1. **Location name as header** — "Mariana Trench.", "Lake Vostok.", "Verlikina Cave."
2. **One terrifying dimension stat** — "Seven miles of water above the bottom"
3. **Stack unexplained mysteries** — "Scientists don't know...", "Something is moving...", "No known species matches..."
4. **Sensory/visceral descriptions** — "The water is thick, almost syrup-like"
5. **End each section with an open question** — "The trench might contain intelligent life we've never documented"

### Script Characteristics
- Short, punchy sentences. No fluff.
- Each paragraph is 2-3 sentences max.
- Heavy use of "Something..." and mystery language.
- Reads like a thriller, not a textbook.
- Every section escalates — each location more mysterious than the last.
