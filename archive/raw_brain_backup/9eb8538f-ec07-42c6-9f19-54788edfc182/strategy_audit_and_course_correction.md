### c) "Start Messy" vs "Pick Your Niche" — What Works in 2025-2026?

**"Start Messy" wins for channels below 10K subs. Here's why:**

The YouTube algorithm doesn't care about your niche until you have audience signals. For your first 10-20 videos, the algorithm is testing:
- Does anyone click this? (CTR)
- Does anyone watch this? (APV)
- Does anyone engage? (likes, comments, shares)

It tests these PER VIDEO, not per channel. Your channel's "niche coherence" only starts mattering when YouTube decides what audience clusters to push you to — and that requires enough videos and data for the system to identify your audience.

**The evidence:** Looking at the fresh outlier data, the channels that broke out in the last 90 days have generic names: "The Fact Explainer," "Explain Nest," "WiseGlobe," "Knowing Noah," "ExplainTory." None of these sound like they spent weeks agonizing over sub-niche positioning. They sound like someone who said "I'll explain things" and started.
