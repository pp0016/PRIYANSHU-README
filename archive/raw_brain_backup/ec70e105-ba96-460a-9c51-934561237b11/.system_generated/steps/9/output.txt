Bark Technologies operates primarily as a **B2C (Business-to-Consumer)** company[1] providing parental control and digital safety services through a **subscription-based model**[2][3].

### Purchasing and Subscription Model
*   **Core Services:** Bark’s primary software offerings are subscription-based[2][3]. Customers can choose between monthly or annual billing cycles for plans like **Bark Jr.** and **Bark Premium**[2][4]. 
*   **Hardware Integration:** Bark also offers hardware products, such as the **Bark Phone**, **Bark Watch**, and **Bark Home**[5][6][2].
    *   **Bark Phone/Watch:** These devices are typically purchased via a monthly installment plan (e.g., 24 months)[5][6]. Importantly, these devices require a separate, active service/wireless plan to function[6][7]. 
    *   **Bark Home:** This is a physical device for Wi-Fi filtering that can be leased or purchased; it also requires an active Bark subscription to operate[2][4].
*   **Bundling:** The Bark Phone and wireless plans often include a Bark Premium subscription for the entire family, covering unlimited devices[5][6][7].

### Retail and Availability
*   **Direct-to-Consumer (DTC):** Most Bark Technologies services and devices are sold directly through their official website[8]. 
*   **Retail:** Certain hardware, such as the Bark Phone, may be available through major retailers like Amazon or Walmart[5]. 

*Note: Please do not confuse "Bark Technologies" (the parental safety company)[9] with "BARK" (the company known for BarkBox, a subscription service for dog toys and treats)[10][11]. While both use subscription models[12][2], they operate in entirely different industries[1][10].*
