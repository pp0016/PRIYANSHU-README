### How the agents built this:
- **Mesh Builder** wrote code to generate the head, torso, and individual limbs as 3D geometry mathematically matching your 2D sketch.
- **Material Artist** applied an "Inverted Hull" toon shader to create a white fill with thick black outlines, flattening the 3D model to look perfectly 2D.
- **Rigger** built an armature (skeleton) and bound the geometry to it so the arms and legs could bend independently.
- **Animator** keyed a walking cycle (arms swinging opposite to legs) and a laughing fit (bending the spine, holding the belly).
- **Renderer** used an orthographic camera to render all 480 frames perfectly flat!
