# What vidIQ's 3 Locked Features Actually Do — And Your Free Replacement Stack

**Date:** 2026-08-04 | **Depth:** Quick | **Sources:** 10+ across both agents

---

## 1. SUBSCRIBER INSIGHTS — What's Inside

Shows **what your subscribers watch on OTHER channels** and **which other channels they subscribe to**. That's it. Two data panels.

**Can you get this free?** ✅ **YES — YouTube Studio already does this.**
Go to YouTube Studio → Analytics → Audience tab. You'll see:
- "What your audience watches" (videos across YouTube)
- "Channels your audience watches" (other channels they follow)

vidIQ just reskins this same data under a "Subscriber" label. YouTube Studio's version is actually **more accurate** because it's first-party data, not estimates.

**Verdict: Not worth paying for. You already have it.**

## 2. VIDEO OPTIMIZATIONS — What's Inside

A bundle of 6 things:
1. **AI Title Generator** — suggests high-CTR titles
2. **AI Description Writer** — writes optimized descriptions
3. **SEO Scorecard** — real-time score while uploading (tags, keywords, title length)
4. **Tag Suggestions** — recommends tags from top-performing similar videos
5. **Keyword Volume + Competition scores** — proprietary YouTube-specific search data
6. **AI Script Writer** — turns ideas into scripts

**Can you get this free?** ✅ **Mostly YES.**

| What vidIQ does | Free replacement |
|---|---|
| AI Titles | ChatGPT / Gemini (any AI chatbot) |
| AI Descriptions | ChatGPT / Gemini |
| SEO Scorecard | TubeBuddy free tier |
| Tag Suggestions | TubeBuddy free tier |
| Script Writing | ChatGPT / Gemini |
| **Keyword Volume/Competition** | **❌ No free equivalent** — Google Trends shows *relative* interest (not numbers), keywordtool.io gives keyword *ideas* without volume data |

**The one thing you can't fully replicate free:** vidIQ's YouTube-specific search volume + competition numbers. Google Trends (filter → YouTube Search) gets you 70% there by showing relative interest, but doesn't give exact monthly search counts.

**Verdict: 5 out of 6 sub-features are free elsewhere. The keyword data is the only unique value.**

## 3. THUMBNAIL AI — What's Inside

An AI image generator built into vidIQ's dashboard. You give it a text prompt or video URL, it generates thumbnail concepts. Has text overlays, face swapping, and basic editing.

**Can you get this free?** ✅ **YES — and better tools exist.**

| Free Tool | What it does | Why it's better than vidIQ's |
|---|---|---|
| **Canva** (canva.com) | Full design editor, 1000s of YouTube thumbnail templates, basic AI features | Far more polished output, full design control, brand kits |
| **Adobe Express** (adobe.com/express) | Professional templates, AI background removal, text effects | Higher quality output |
| **WayinVideo** (wayin.ai) | Uploads your video, generates thumbnails from actual frames — "promptless" | More contextual to your actual video content |
| **Vidocu** (vidocu.ai) | Pull stills from video + AI thumbnail design | Video-first approach |
| **ChatGPT / GPT Image Gen** | Generate thumbnail concepts from text prompts | More creative control |

**Verdict: Not worth paying for. Canva free alone beats vidIQ's thumbnail AI.**

## Your Multi-Account Strategy — Will It Work?

You said: "I'll make 5 vidIQ accounts, login daily for +20 credits each, and use them alternatively."

**Problems with this:**
1. **Credits don't transfer between accounts** — you can't pool 5 accounts into one workspace
2. **Each account is tied to ONE YouTube channel** — connecting the same channel to multiple accounts may violate ToS
3. **The 3 features above (Subscriber Insights, Video Optimizations, Thumbnail AI) are FEATURE-LOCKED, not credit-locked** — even with infinite credits on a free account, these features don't appear in your dashboard
4. **You'd be managing 5 separate logins daily** for ~100 usable credits total (after tool costs eat into the 20/day). That's a lot of friction for minimal gain.

**It's not a credit problem. It's a feature-gate problem.**

## YOUR FREE "PREMIUM" STACK — 6 Tools, ₹0/month

Here's the combination that replaces ~80% of vidIQ Boost:

| Need | Free Tool | URL |
|---|---|---|
| **Audience Insights** | YouTube Studio (Audience tab) | studio.youtube.com |
| **Keyword Research** | Google Trends (YouTube filter) + keywordtool.io | trends.google.com / keywordtool.io/youtube |
| **SEO Score + Tags** | TubeBuddy (free tier) | tubebuddy.com |
| **Titles/Descriptions/Scripts** | ChatGPT or Gemini | chatgpt.com / gemini.google.com |
| **Thumbnails** | Canva (free) + WayinVideo | canva.com / wayin.ai |
| **Competitor Tracking** | Social Blade | socialblade.com |

### What's Missing (the 20% you can't get free):
- **Exact YouTube keyword search volume numbers** (Google Trends gives relative data only)
- **Automated daily trend alerts specific to your niche** (you'd have to manually check Google Trends)
- **Bulk video optimization** (doing SEO on 10+ videos at once — irrelevant at your current scale)
