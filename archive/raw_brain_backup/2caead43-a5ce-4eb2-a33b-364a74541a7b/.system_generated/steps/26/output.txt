The **Gabb Phone 4 Pro** is a specialized, kid-friendly smartphone that is effectively a highly modified **Samsung Galaxy A15 5G**[1][2]. Because it is built on this hardware foundation, it offers more advanced specifications compared to the standard Gabb Phone 4[1][3].

### **Key Specifications**

*   **Hardware Foundation:** The device is built using the Samsung Galaxy A15 5G[1][2]. It runs a customized version of Android 14 with Gabb’s proprietary safety and parental control software[1][2].
*   **Camera:** 
    *   **Rear:** Triple-camera system featuring a **50MP** main sensor, a 5MP ultra-wide lens, and a 2MP macro lens[1][4].
    *   **Front:** 13MP camera for video calls and selfies[1][4].
*   **Battery:** It is equipped with a high-capacity battery that typically provides all-day performance, often lasting 1–2 days depending on usage[5][1].
*   **RAM & Storage:**
    *   **Storage:** 128GB of internal storage[4].
    *   **RAM:** While official documentation often focuses on the user experience, as a Samsung Galaxy A15 derivative, it typically features 4GB or more of RAM (standard for that hardware configuration)[5].

### **Safety & Usage**
The Gabb Phone 4 Pro is designed for older teens (ages 13–18)[1][3]. Unlike standard smartphones, it does not have a traditional app store, social media access, or an open internet browser[6][1][2]. Instead, it allows parents to selectively enable specific, Gabb-approved third-party apps[7][3][8]. It also features robust parental controls, including real-time GPS tracking and AI-powered content filtration for messaging[6][1][9].
