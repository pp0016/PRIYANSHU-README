Bark Technologies announced a **$30 million Series C funding round** on June 1, 2022[1][2][3]. The round was led by **Kayne Partners**, the growth private equity group of Kayne Anderson Capital Advisors, with participation from existing investors[2][4][5].

### Key Details:
*   **Funding Amount:** $30 million[1][2][3].
*   **Total Funding:** This round brought Bark Technologies' total funding raised to date to **$67 million**[2][6][7].
*   **Purpose:** The company stated the funds would be used to accelerate international expansion, establish strategic global partnerships, and further its position in the online safety space[2][4][5].
*   **Board Changes:** As part of the investment, Leon Chen, Managing Partner at Kayne Partners, joined the company's board of directors[2][3][4].

**Valuation Information:**
Bark Technologies is a private company[8]. While some financial platforms estimate its valuation range (e.g., Dealroom has historically estimated a range of $100–500 million)[9], the specific post-money valuation from the Series C round has not been publicly disclosed by the company[10].
