### "Open Weights" vs. "Open Source"
*   **Open Weights:** Llama provides access to the model's trained parameters (weights), allowing developers to run, fine-tune, and integrate the model into products. This is the primary value for most businesses.
*   **Not Fully Open Source:** Because the license imposes field-of-use restrictions (e.g., the MAU cap, prohibitions on training other models), it fails to meet the "freedom to use" and "no discrimination against fields of endeavor" criteria required for a model to be considered true open source.
