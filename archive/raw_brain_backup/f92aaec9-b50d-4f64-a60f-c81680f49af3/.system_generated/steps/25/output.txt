### 2. When Local is Still "Better"

Users working in B2B or specialized fields often find that major cloud providers (ChatGPT, Gemini) "poison" conversations with overly restrictive guardrails, forcing them back to uncensored or local models to get actual work done[6].
*   **Cost Efficiency at Scale:** For tasks requiring billions of tokens, users with significant local hardware report that it is significantly cheaper to run local models than to pay per-token API costs[1].
*   **Privacy and Agency:** High-performance local models (such as certain Qwen or specialized MoE variants) are still cited as "beasts" that can handle complex tasks—like reverse engineering or multi-file coding—without the risk of privacy leaks or external policy interference[7][1][8].

The consensus in 2026 is that **local LLMs are no longer a "toy"** but remain a specialized tool[1][3].
