## Professional Adjustments Applied

### 1. Background: Cream + Localized Dark Aura
The cream `#F2EDE4` stays as the main background — this is what makes it POP in YouTube's dark feed. BUT behind the head silhouette, a soft dark warm vignette `#2A1A10` at ~40% opacity creates a "glow-friendly zone." The brain glow needs SOME darkness to shine against. Think of it as: the head is so powerful it's creating its own atmosphere.

### 2. Growth Blocks: Gold Gradient, Not Multicolor
The corporate multicolor blocks from Image 2 break the visual harmony. Instead:
- ALL blocks use a **warm gold monochrome** palette
- Smallest blocks: pale gold `#E8D5A8` (faded, quiet)
- Medium blocks: warm amber `#D4A030` (growing intensity)
- Largest blocks: bright gold `#FFAA00` with a subtle glow effect
- The biggest 2-3 blocks near the head start radiating golden light particles — visually CONNECTING to the brain glow
- This creates a visual energy flow: pale → warm → glowing → BRAIN EXPLOSION

### 3. Head Silhouette: Warm Brown, Not Pure Black
On cream, pure black feels pasted. Using dark warm brown `#2A1A10` integrates the silhouette into the warm palette. The head faces LEFT (toward the growth blocks), showing it's RECEIVING the compound growth.

### 4. Brain Glow: Intensified + Connected
The brain glow from Image 1 was good but isolated. Now:
- The glow connects to the largest growth blocks via golden particles/energy trails
- Golden light rays from the brain spill slightly onto the cream background
- Ember particles rise from the top of the head
- The glow intensity: center of brain = white-hot `#FFF5D0`, mid-glow = bright gold `#FFAA00`, outer glow = warm amber `#CC7700`

### 5. Text: Size-Scaling Along the Curve
- "TINY" (physically small font ~3% frame height) positioned near the smallest blocks
- Arrow "→" connecting them along the growth curve
- "MASSIVE" (physically LARGE font ~12% frame height) near the biggest blocks
- Font: bold charcoal `#2A1A10` on the cream background
- The font size literally demonstrates the message — it GROWS as you read

### 6. Element Hierarchy
| Element | Visual Weight | Attention % |
|---|---|---|
| Glowing Head | DOMINANT — largest, brightest | 40% |
| Growth Blocks | SECONDARY — visual bridge, movement | 35% |
| Book | TERTIARY — anchor, source identity | 20% |
| Text | SUPPORTING — labels the curve | 5% |

**Curiosity Gap:** A mind is GLOWING — but what caused it? The growth blocks show something COMPOUNDED from the book into the brain. You have to listen to find out what.

**Four C's:**
| C | Status |
|---|---|
| **Clarity** | ✅ Three elements, one dominant (glowing head), clear left-to-right story |
| **Contrast** | ✅ Cream background creates feed contrast. Dark head aura + golden glow creates internal contrast |
| **Consistency** | ✅ Gold monochrome palette unifies all three elements into one composition |
| **Character** | ✅ The glowing brain IS the emotional hook — "I want my brain to glow like that" |

**Mobile Test (120px):** At thumb size, viewer sees — bright golden glow point on the right (stops the scroll) + recognizable book shape on the left (identifies the content). The growth blocks are too small to read at thumb size but they create a visual BRIDGE that moves the eye from left to right.

**The Scroller:** "I see a BRIGHT GOLDEN GLOW on a cream background in my dark YouTube feed. That's a hard contrast. I stop. I see a book shape, some kind of growth, and a head with a brain lit up. This book does something to your mind. I'm curious. Click."
