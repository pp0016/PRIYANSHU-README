## 5. Key Formulas to Memorize

```
── Unit 2 ──────────────────────────────────────────
Power Factor (PF)      = kW / kVA  =  cos φ
Load Factor (LF)       = Average Load / Maximum Demand
Demand Factor (DF)     = Maximum Demand / Total Connected Load
Diversity Factor       = Sum of Individual Max Demands / Coincident Max Demand
SEC                    = Total Energy Consumed (kWh) / Total Output (units)

── Unit 3 ──────────────────────────────────────────
THD (current)          = √(I₃² + I₅² + I₇² + ...) / I₁ × 100%
kVAr required          = P × (tan φ₁ − tan φ₂)
                         where φ₁ = old PF angle, φ₂ = desired PF angle
Capacitance            = kVAr / (2π f V²) × 10⁹  (in µF, single phase)
Loss reduction (%)     = [1 − (PF_old / PF_new)²] × 100

── Unit 4 ──────────────────────────────────────────
Affinity Laws (pumps/fans):
  Q₂/Q₁ = N₂/N₁           (flow ∝ speed)
  H₂/H₁ = (N₂/N₁)²        (head ∝ speed²)
  P₂/P₁ = (N₂/N₁)³        (power ∝ speed³)

Motor energy savings   = P_rated × [(1/η_old) − (1/η_new)] × hours × ₹/kWh
Solar PV annual yield  = P_rated (kWp) × CUF × 8760 hours
Performance Ratio (PR) = Actual Energy Output / (Irradiation × Panel Area × η_STC)
```
