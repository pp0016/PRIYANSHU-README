### Summary for Local Deployment (2026)
*   **For Low-End Hardware (e.g., 8GB VRAM):** You can comfortably run **Whisper Small/Medium** and a lightweight TTS model like **Kokoro-82M** or **Chatterbox**. An RTX 3060 Ti (8GB) is widely considered sufficient for these setups.
*   **For High-Performance/Production:** If you require real-time, low-latency performance with advanced features like voice cloning (e.g., **VoxCPM2** or **Zonos**), a GPU with **12 GB to 24 GB of VRAM** (such as an RTX 3090, 4090, or 5090) is recommended to handle the combined load of the transcription and speech synthesis engines.
*   **Engineers' Tip:** Always account for your "KV Cache" and model overhead when calculating VRAM. Even if a model's weights fit, you need additional VRAM (typically 1–2 GB) for the inference engine (e.g., SGLang, vLLM, or llama.cpp) to process requests efficiently.
