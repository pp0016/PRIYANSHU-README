Gabb provides several layers of parental control and safety features across its devices and services, including spam blocking, app management, and content monitoring.

### **Blocking & Spam Protection**
*   **Gabb Guard:** This is a built-in spam-blocking technology that automatically identifies and blocks suspected spam calls and texts before they reach the device[1][2]. Unlike strict contact-list filters, it aims to block suspicious activity while still allowing most unrecognized (but legitimate) callers to get through[2].
*   **Gabb Messenger:** Uses "proprietary smart filtering" to block or flag explicit images, links, and harmful language in messages[3]. If content is blocked or flagged, parents receive a notification via the **Gabb Messenger for Parents** app[3][1].
*   **Video Calls:** Gabb Messenger features safe video calling. If nudity is detected during a call, the system will end the call, block video calling, and notify the parent[4][5].

### **App Management & Third-Party Apps**
*   **Strict Approval Process:** Gabb employs a curated, kid-safe app marketplace[6]. Third-party apps undergo a "strict approval process" regarding content, functionality, safety, privacy, and in-app purchase features before they are made available[6].
*   **Device Limitations:** Certain devices, such as the Gabb Phone 4, have restricted access to apps, while the Gabb Phone 4 Pro on the "Advanced Plan" provides access to a broader library of curated third-party apps[6].
*   **App Restrictions:** Parents can request new apps through their Gabb account, though not all requests are approved[6]. Users have reported challenges with certain third-party apps that may still allow navigation to unauthorized web content (like Google Search) via links within those apps[7].

### **Notifications & Monitoring**
*   **Parental Notifications:** Gabb devices and apps are designed to send notifications to the parent's smartphone for various events, including blocked/flagged content[3][1], detected nudity in video calls[4], and certain system-level privacy notifications (e.g., location access)[8].
*   **Contact Management:** On compatible devices, parents can add, approve, and manage contacts for their child[3]. While watches are restricted to approved contacts, parents have noted that, on some phones, incoming calls from unapproved numbers may still occasionally reach the device despite these settings[3][1].
