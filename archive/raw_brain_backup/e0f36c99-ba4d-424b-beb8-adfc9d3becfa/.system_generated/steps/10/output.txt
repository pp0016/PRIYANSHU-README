The current base model for the Bark Phone is the **Samsung Galaxy A16**[1][4]. Previous iterations have utilized other models such as the Samsung Galaxy A14 5G[5] and older A-series devices[6].

**General Specifications (A16 / Recent Models):**
*   **Operating System:** Android 14[7] (varies by model year).
*   **Display:** 6.7 inches[7].
*   **Resolution:** 1080 x 2400[7].
*   **Processor (CPU):** 2.16 GHz[7].
*   **RAM:** 4 GB[7].
*   **Storage:** 128 GB[7].
*   **Battery:** 4900 mAh (approximate)[7].
*   **Connectivity:** Supports 4G LTE and 5G (depending on network and signal availability)[8][9].

### **Bark Phone Pro**
The **Bark Phone Pro** is a more robust device designed for older children and teens who need higher performance[3]. It is based on the **Samsung Galaxy A36 5G**[10][1].

**Key Hardware Highlights:**
*   **Processor:** Octa-core processor[11] (Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 3)[10].
*   **RAM:** 6 GB[11].
*   **Display:** 6.7-inch screen with a faster refresh rate[10].
*   **Durability:** Features Gorilla Glass for crack resistance and **IP67 water and dust resistance**[11][1].
