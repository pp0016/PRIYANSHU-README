## 4. 🎯 Strategy A: 20/30 Marks (~6-8 hrs)
*A 30-mark exam will heavily favor derivations and 3-mark definitions over 45-minute graphical numericals.*

1. **Vibration Definitions (1 hour):** Memorize the definitions and draw the graphs for: Coefficient of fluctuation of speed/energy, Magnification Factor, Transmissibility, Under/Over/Critically damped curves, Resonance. (Guaranteed 3-6 marks).
2. **Logarithmic Decrement (1.5 hours):** Memorize the mathematical derivation and practice 2 short numericals using $\delta = \ln(x_1/x_2) = \frac{2\pi\zeta}{\sqrt{1-\zeta^2}}$.
3. **Natural Frequency Methods (1.5 hours):** Learn how to derive the equation of motion for a simple spring-mass system using the Energy Method and Rayleigh's Method. 
4. **Gyroscope on a Ship (1 hour):** Memorize the formula $C = I \cdot \omega \cdot \omega_p$. Understand the effect of Pitching, Steering, and Rolling. Do ONE numerical where the ship pitches.
5. **Static vs Dynamic Balancing (0.5 hours):** Memorize the theoretical difference and conditions required for both.

## 5. 🎯 Strategy B: 25/30 Marks (~10-12 hrs)
*Complete Strategy A, then add:*

1. **Piston Effort Derivation (1.5 hours):** Derive turning moment at the crankshaft in terms of piston effort.
2. **Flywheel & Punching Press Numericals (1.5 hours):** Practice finding the mass of a flywheel given energy fluctuation ($\Delta E = I \cdot \omega^2 \cdot C_s$) and the punching press cycle.
3. **Torsional Vibrations (1 hour):** Learn the formula for equivalent shaft length and the node position for a 2-rotor system.
4. **Dynamically Equivalent System (0.5 hours):** Memorize the 3 conditions ($m = m_1+m_2$, $L_1m_1 = L_2m_2$, $I = m_1L_1^2 + m_2L_2^2$).

## 6. Key Formulas to Memorize

**Vibrations:**
*   Natural frequency: $\omega_n = \sqrt{k/m}$
*   Critical damping coefficient: $c_c = 2\sqrt{km} = 2m\omega_n$
*   Damping factor / ratio: $\zeta = c / c_c$
*   Logarithmic decrement: $\delta = \frac{2\pi\zeta}{\sqrt{1-\zeta^2}} = \frac{1}{n} \ln(\frac{x_1}{x_{n+1}})$

**Gyroscope:**
*   Gyroscopic Couple: $C = I \cdot \omega \cdot \omega_p$
*   Precession angular velocity (Pitching SHM): $\omega_p = \Phi \cdot \frac{2\pi}{t_p} \cdot \cos(\frac{2\pi}{t_p}t)$

**Flywheel:**
*   Max Fluctuation of Energy: $\Delta E = \frac{\pi^2}{900} \cdot m \cdot k^2 \cdot N^2 \cdot C_s$
*   Coefficient of Fluctuation of Speed: $C_s = \frac{N_1 - N_2}{N_{mean}}$
