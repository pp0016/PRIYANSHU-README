*   **In-Store Bargaining:** Some customers have reported success in negotiating prices at physical Vijay Sales branches, especially when providing a valid student ID[5]. If you choose this route, it is advisable to visit the store in person with your physical college ID card and compare quotes from multiple branches or nearby competing retailers like Reliance Digital[5].
*   **No-Cost EMI:** Vijay Sales often provides competitive No-Cost EMI options, which can sometimes be more financially advantageous than the standard EMI options available through the Apple Education Store[6][7].

**Recommendation:**
If your primary goal is to get the official Apple education pricing (which often includes additional promotional items like free AirPods or Apple Pencil during the annual Back to School season), it is generally best to purchase directly through the **official Apple Education Store**[7][8]. 

If you prefer the convenience of local service, immediate pickup, or specific EMI offers, check the current bank promotions on the [Vijay Sales website](https://www.vijaysales.com) and call your local branch to ask if they can offer a special price for students[9][5][10].
