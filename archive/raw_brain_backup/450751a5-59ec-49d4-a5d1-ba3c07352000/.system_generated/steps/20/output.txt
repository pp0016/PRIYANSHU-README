Student discounts for MacBooks are available through major Apple Premium Resellers in India, such as **Aptronix** and **Imagine**, as well as through Apple's official education pricing[1][2][3].

### **Aptronix**
Aptronix offers a dedicated **Aptronix Student Deals (ASD)** program[1].
*   **Benefits:** Includes flat percentage discounts on MacBooks (e.g., 8–10% off depending on the model), flat discounts on Apple accessories (5%) and third-party accessories (15%), and savings on AppleCare+ services[4][5][6].
*   **Additional Perks:** They often provide bank cashback offers and "Lifestyle and Education" vouchers[4][5][6].
*   **How to Avail:** You can explore their student collection online or visit an Aptronix store with your student/UNiDAYS ID for verification[7][8].

### **Imagine**
The Imagine Store provides education pricing for students and faculty[9][2].
*   **Benefits:** Special pricing on MacBook Air and MacBook Pro models[9][10]. They also offer no-cost EMI and cashback options[2].
*   **How to Avail:** You are typically required to verify your student or faculty status[10]. This often involves submitting a consent form along with proof of your student ID, admission letter, or marksheet via their online portal[10].

### **Unicorn**
Unicorn, another major Apple Premium Partner, offers student-focused promotions[11].
*   **Offers:** While they frequently run "Hot Deals" and seasonal sales (sometimes offering significant discounts on older or specific models), they also participate in Apple’s standard education pricing programs[11][12].
*   **Note:** It is recommended to check their website or contact a local store directly to see the latest specific student discount rates, as these can fluctuate based on ongoing promotional cycles[11][13].

### **Maple**
Maple Store is known for its focus on certified pre-owned Apple devices, which can be a cost-effective alternative for students[14][15].
*   **Offers:** While they offer sales on pre-owned MacBooks, they also provide access to official Apple education benefits for new devices[14][16].

### **Important Tips for Students**
*   **Verify Eligibility:** Always have your official student ID, college email address, or admission documentation ready, as these are standard requirements for verification[10].
*   **Compare Prices:** Check the **official Apple India Education Store** online first[17]. Apple's direct education discount is often the benchmark (typically around 10% off), and they frequently include seasonal offers like free or discounted accessories (e.g., AirPods or Apple Pencil)[17][18][19].
*   **Combine Offers:** Look for opportunities to stack bank cashback offers on top of the base student discount to maximize your savings[4][6][2].
