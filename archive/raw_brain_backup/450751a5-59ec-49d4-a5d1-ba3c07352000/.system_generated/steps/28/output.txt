### **Key Information for Education Pricing in India:**

*   **Eligibility:** You are eligible for education pricing if you are[1]:
    *   A student currently attending or accepted into a tertiary education institution[1].
    *   A parent purchasing on behalf of a student at a tertiary institution[1].
    *   An employee (teacher or staff) of any public or private education institution in India[1].
*   **Benefits:**
    *   **Education Savings:** Enjoy special pricing on Mac models, including the MacBook Pro[2][3].
    *   **College Student Offers:** During specific promotional periods (such as the annual "Back to School" promotion), eligible purchasers may receive additional benefits, such as promotional savings on accessories like AirPods or AirTags when purchasing an eligible Mac[3][4].
    *   **Pro Apps Bundle:** Students and educators can purchase a pre-installed bundle of pro apps (Final Cut Pro, Motion, Compressor, Logic Pro, and MainStage) at a special education rate[2].
*   **Verification:** You may be required to verify your status as a student or educator to qualify for these discounts[5].
*   **Additional Savings:** You can also take advantage of **Apple Trade In** to trade in your current device for credit toward your new MacBook Pro[6].
