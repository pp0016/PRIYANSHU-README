In its deep-dive analysis of the Apple M1 chip, AnandTech measured the memory latency to be approximately **96 nanoseconds** at a 128MB full random test depth[1][2]. 

This measurement was noted as being higher (i.e., slower) than the latency observed in contemporary x86 counterparts, such as the AMD Ryzen 9 5950X, which AnandTech measured at roughly 78.8ns in the DRAM region[1][2]. The analysis highlighted this performance characteristic as a point of comparison when evaluating Apple's unified memory architecture against traditional desktop processor designs[3][1].
