Find out about your options for AppleCare coverage for your Apple device.
AppleCare plans available for your device and how to buy AppleCare may differ based on your country or region and the device you want to cover. [Find information about AppleCare pricing and benefits in your country or region](https://www.apple.com/applecare/)

AppleCare Protection Plan coverage is available to buy when you purchase your device and within 12 months after purchase.

Your AppleCare coverage options include the AppleCare Protection Plan and AppleCare+.

### AppleCare Protection Plan
AppleCare Protection Plan coverage is available to buy when you purchase your device and within 12 months after purchase.

### AppleCare+
AppleCare+ coverage is available to buy when you purchase your device.

Your AppleCare coverage option includes AppleCare+. If your AppleCare plan for your Apple device expires, you may be able to purchase new coverage within 45 days after your coverage ends. Your new plan will have recurring payments.

When you purchase a new Apple device, and within 60 days of purchase, you can add AppleCare+ coverage.

You can get AppleCare coverage in Settings on an iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple Vision Pro associated with your Apple Account. [Open AppleCare & Warranty settings on your device](https://coverage.apple.com/cc/acW4820), or follow these steps:
1. Open Settings on your device.
2. Select General.
3. Select AppleCare & Warranty.

## Available AppleCare plans
Your AppleCare coverage options include AppleCare+, AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss and AppleCare One. If your AppleCare plan for your Apple device expires, you may be able to purchase new coverage within 45 days after your coverage ends. Your new plan will have recurring payments.

### AppleCare+
Four days after your Apple device is delivered or picked up from Apple, and within 60 days of your purchase, you can buy AppleCare+ for your device.

### AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss
Four days after your Apple device is delivered or picked up from Apple, and within 60 days of your purchase, you can buy AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss for your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch.

### AppleCare One
Four days after your Apple device is delivered or picked up from Apple, and within 60 days of your purchase, you can buy an AppleCare One plan. You'll start with coverage for up to three devices, and you can swap devices and add more devices to your AppleCare One plan. For iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, Theft and Loss coverage is included in your AppleCare One plan.
[Find out how to start an AppleCare One plan](https://support.apple.com/127207)

Your AppleCare coverage options include AppleCare+, AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss and AppleCare One. If your AppleCare plan for your Apple device expires, you may be able to purchase new coverage within 45 days after your coverage ends. Your new plan will have recurring payments.

### AppleCare+
When you purchase a new Mac, Apple Vision Pro, Apple Display, Apple TV, HomePod, AirPods or Beats, and within 60 days of purchase, you can add AppleCare+ coverage that renews monthly or annually.

### AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss
When you purchase a new iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch, and within 60 days of purchase, you can add AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss coverage that renews monthly or annually.

### AppleCare One
When you purchase a new product from Apple, you can buy an AppleCare One plan. If you don't buy AppleCare One when you purchase your new device, you have 60 days after purchase to buy an AppleCare One plan using your iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple Vision Pro. You'll start with coverage for up to three devices, and you can [add more devices to your AppleCare One plan](https://support.apple.com/122224) at any time. For iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, Theft and Loss coverage is included in your AppleCare One plan.
[Find out how to start an AppleCare One plan](https://support.apple.com/127207)
