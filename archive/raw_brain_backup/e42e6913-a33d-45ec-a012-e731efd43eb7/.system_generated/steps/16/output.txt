YouTube provides specific procedures for individuals to request the removal of AI-generated or synthetic content that simulates their face or voice[1][2][3]. These protections are part of YouTube's privacy guidelines and are distinct from copyright claims[1][4][5].

### **How to Request Removal**
If you believe a video on YouTube contains an AI-generated or synthetic version of your face or voice, you can use the platform's **privacy request process**[1][6][7].

1.  **Likeness Detection (For Creators):** If you are a creator (18+), you can set up **Likeness Detection** in YouTube Studio[8][9]. This tool scans newly uploaded videos for your face and provides a dashboard where you can review matches and initiate removal requests[8][9][10].
2.  **Privacy Complaint Process:** For those not using the automated likeness detection tool, or for concerns regarding voice simulation (which is currently primarily handled via manual reports), you can follow the standard **Privacy Complaint Process**[8][1][10].
3.  **What YouTube Considers:** When evaluating these requests, YouTube considers several factors[1][4][11]:
    *   Whether the content is realistic and could be mistaken for the actual person[1][6][12].
    *   Whether the content is clearly disclosed as AI-generated[4][6].
    *   Whether the content constitutes parody, satire, or has public interest value[1][6][11].
    *   Whether the individual is identifiable[1][3].

### **Important Details**
*   **Response Time:** After a complaint is filed, the uploader is typically given **48 hours** to remove or edit the content (e.g., using blur tools) before YouTube initiates a manual review[4][13][3].
*   **Not a Copyright Strike:** Privacy violations are separate from Community Guidelines strikes[1][5]. A removal request under the privacy policy does not automatically result in a copyright strike against the uploader, though repeated violations may lead to account-level action[4][14][3].
*   **Eligibility:** Generally, these are "first-party" claims, meaning the request must be made by the person whose likeness is being simulated (or their legal representative/guardian)[3].
*   **Transparency Requirements:** YouTube also mandates that creators disclose when they use AI to create "realistic" content that might mislead viewers[12][15][16]. Failing to disclose such content can lead to penalties, including content removal or suspension from the YouTube Partner Program[17][18].

For the most up-to-date options, log in to your **YouTube Studio** dashboard if you are a creator, or visit the **YouTube Help Center** to navigate the specific reporting forms for privacy violations[8][6][10].
