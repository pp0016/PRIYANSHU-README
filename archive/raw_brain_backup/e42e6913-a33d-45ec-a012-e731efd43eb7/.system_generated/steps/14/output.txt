YouTube has significantly tightened its monetization policies throughout 2025 and 2026 to prioritize **original, high-value, and human-driven content**[1][2]. The shift from "repetitious content" to the broader **"inauthentic content"** policy is a direct response to the surge in mass-produced, low-effort AI videos[1][3][2].

Niches like **horror narration, history facts, quizzes, and meditation** are currently facing high rates of demonetization because they are frequently produced using templates, stock footage, and AI-generated voices, which often fail to demonstrate the "creative fingerprint" YouTube now demands[4][5][6][7].

### Key Reasons for Demonetization
*   **Lack of "Transformative" Value:** YouTube’s algorithms and human reviewers look for significant original input[8]. Simply compiling stock footage, using text-to-speech, and adding background music is no longer considered "transformative"[4][9][7].
*   **Inauthentic Content (The "AI Slop" Purge):** This policy targets content that is mass-produced, follows a rigid template with minimal variation, or lacks clear human author input[3][2]. If your videos feel indistinguishable from hundreds of other channels in your niche, they are at high risk[7].
*   **Reused Content:** This remains a major hurdle if you are using footage, audio, or stories from other sources without adding substantial original commentary, critical analysis, or your own unique narrative voice[9][8].
*   **Automated Flagging:** Much of the initial detection is done by AI[10]. Channels are often "false flagged," but once a channel is under review, human moderators look for evidence of genuine human effort—such as your own voice, face, or unique editing style[1][11][10].

### Risks by Niche
*   **Horror/History:** Many channels in these niches are being hit because they rely heavily on "stock" scary stories or historical facts narrated by AI voices with generic AI-generated visuals[4][5][6].
*   **Quiz Channels:** These are highly susceptible to "inauthentic content" flags if they follow a basic Q&A template without unique host personality or deep, original educational commentary[12][2].
*   **Meditation/Relaxation:** These channels are often flagged if they use non-original visuals, generic AI-generated ambient music, or repetitive loops that provide little original value[7][8].

### How to Protect Your Channel
*   **Add Your "Creative Fingerprint":** YouTube needs to see that a real person is behind the content[7]. Use your own voice (not AI), show your face if possible, and include unique personal insights or original research[11][7].
*   **Transform, Don't Just Aggregate:** If you must use clips or stock footage, ensure it is heavily edited, narrated, and used to support an original point of view rather than serving as the primary content[13][9][10].
*   **Avoid Templates:** Move away from "systemized" content generation[7]. Even if you use AI tools for assistance, your final product should not look like it was produced by a factory-style script[3][2].
*   **Appeal Strategy:** If you are demonetized, focus your appeal on demonstrating the **human effort** involved[14][11]. Explain the creative process, how you write your scripts, and how your content provides unique value that cannot be replicated by an AI prompt[6][7].
