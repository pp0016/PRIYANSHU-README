The choice between these two machines rarely comes down to performance, as they are effectively the same in that regard[1][2][3]. Instead, consider the following practical differences:

*   **Form Factor & Portability:** The MacBook Pro is a self-contained, portable workstation with a best-in-class display, keyboard, and trackpad[10][11]. The Mac mini is a compact desktop that requires you to provide your own peripherals[12][11].
*   **Value:** If you already own high-quality monitors and input devices, the Mac mini offers significantly better value, often saving you hundreds of dollars compared to an equivalent MacBook Pro[10][12][13].
*   **Use Case:** If you need to work from different locations or value the flexibility of an "all-in-one" laptop, the MacBook Pro is the clear choice[10][11]. If your setup is stationary, the Mac mini provides a powerful, space-saving desktop solution[11][3].
