As of 2026, the laptop GPU market is undergoing a significant transition driven by the integration of on-device AI and increasingly demanding graphical workloads. Based on current industry trends and analyst forecasts through 2030, here is the outlook for VRAM capacities.

### VRAM Capacity Trends (2028–2030)

*   **16GB as the New "Sweet Spot":** By 2028, 16GB is widely expected to become the standard baseline for mid-range gaming and creator-focused laptops. While 8GB remains a common entry-level configuration today, it is increasingly viewed as a bottleneck for modern AAA gaming and professional creative tasks. Analysts suggest that 16GB will provide the necessary headroom for the next generation of games and moderate AI-inference workloads.
*   **The 24GB+ Frontier:** While 16GB will likely dominate the mid-range segment, 24GB (and potentially higher) is projected to become the standard for high-end mobile workstations and "enthusiast" gaming laptops. The demand for higher VRAM is being specifically fueled by the need to run local Large Language Models (LLMs) and complex generative AI applications, where model size is often restricted by available VRAM rather than raw compute speed.
*   **Market Segmentation:**
    *   **Entry-Level:** Likely to move toward 8GB–12GB as a floor for basic computing and light media tasks.
    *   **Mid-Range:** Expected to standardize at 16GB to accommodate evolving game engines and common AI-assisted workflows.
    *   **High-End/Professional:** Expected to push into the 24GB+ territory, particularly as mobile hardware continues to replace desktop-class machines for AI development and professional 3D rendering.

### Key Factors Influencing These Trends

*   **Local AI Inference:** This is currently the most significant driver for increased VRAM. Unlike gaming, where VRAM capacity primarily dictates texture quality and resolution, local AI models require large amounts of memory to "fit" the model parameters entirely on the GPU. Users are increasingly prioritizing VRAM capacity over incremental gains in raw compute speed to ensure they can run more capable AI models.
*   **Technological Shifts (GDDR7):** The transition to GDDR7 memory, which is well underway, provides both higher bandwidth and better power efficiency. This shift allows manufacturers to offer higher VRAM densities without the massive thermal penalties previously associated with high-capacity memory modules.
*   **Console-PC Parity:** As next-generation console hardware evolves, PC port requirements generally follow. Analysts note that future game development will likely be optimized for systems with higher memory pools, further pressuring the laptop market to move away from 8GB configurations.
*   **Longevity Concerns:** For consumers looking to purchase a machine that remains "future-proof" through 2030, analysts consistently advise that 16GB of VRAM should be considered the absolute minimum. Those intending to use their laptops for professional creative work or local AI tasks are encouraged to look for 24GB or higher to avoid the performance penalties associated with offloading data to system RAM.
