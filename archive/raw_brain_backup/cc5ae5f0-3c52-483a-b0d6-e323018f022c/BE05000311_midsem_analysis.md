## 6. Key Formulas to Memorize

### Rolling
- **Length of deformation zone:** $l = \sqrt{R \cdot \Delta t}$ where R = roll radius, Δt = draft
- **Draft:** $\Delta t = t_i - t_f$ (initial − final thickness)
- **Maximum draft:** $\Delta t_{max} = \mu^2 \cdot R$ where μ = coefficient of friction
- **Forward slip:** $S_f = \frac{v_f - v_r}{v_r}$ where $v_f$ = exit velocity, $v_r$ = roll surface velocity

### Wire/Tube Drawing
- **Reduction ratio:** $r = \frac{A_0 - A_f}{A_0}$

### Pattern Allowances (values to memorize)
| Material | Shrinkage Allowance (mm/m) |
|---|---|
| Cast iron | 10 |
| Steel | 20 |
| Aluminium | 13 |
| Brass | 15 |

- **Draft allowance:** 1° to 3° (external), 2° to 5° (internal)
- **Machining allowance:** 1.5 to 6 mm depending on size and metal

### Extrusion
- **Extrusion ratio:** $R_e = \frac{A_0}{A_f}$
