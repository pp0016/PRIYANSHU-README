Anthropic has not officially disclosed the exact parameter count for the **Claude Mythos** model series. However, industry reports and expert estimates frequently characterize it as a **~10 trillion parameter model**[1][2][3].

Key details regarding its scale and architecture include:

*   **Estimated Scale:** Most industry analysis and reports place the total parameter count at approximately **8 to 10 trillion**[4][1][5][6]. 
*   **Mixture of Experts (MoE):** It is widely believed that Mythos utilizes a **Mixture of Experts (MoE)** architecture[7][2][6]. This allows the model to possess a massive "knowledge capacity" while maintaining greater computational efficiency, as only a fraction of its total parameters are active during any single forward pass[2][6]. 
*   **Active Parameters:** While the total parameter count is estimated at 10 trillion, researchers have suggested that the number of active parameters per token is significantly lower—likely in the range of **800 billion to 1.2 trillion**—to manage inference latency and compute requirements[2].
*   **Comparison:** For context, this scale represents a significant leap from previous generations of frontier models, such as Claude Opus (often estimated in the 1–2 trillion range)[7][3].

Because Anthropic maintains the model as a proprietary, restricted-access system (often deployed through programs like Project Glasswing), these figures remain based on independent estimates and leaked information rather than official technical specifications[4][8][9].
