Anthropic has not officially disclosed the parameter counts or sizes for any of its models, including **Claude 3 Opus** or **Claude Opus 4.6**[1][2][3].

Because these are proprietary, closed-source models, all figures regarding their parameter counts are third-party estimates or speculations rather than verified technical specifications[4][5][3].

### Summary of Estimates
*   **Claude Opus 4.6:** Estimates for this model have varied significantly due to its complex, likely Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture[4][6]. Some third-party analyses from early 2026 speculated a total size in the range of **~5 trillion parameters**[4][5], though these numbers are approximations based on inference costs, throughput, and hardware-bottleneck comparisons[6][7][8]. Other, more conservative estimates have suggested much lower figures (e.g., ~100B total parameters)[9].
*   **Claude 3 Opus:** Prior to the release of the 4.x series, common estimates for the original Claude 3 Opus also fluctuated, with some analyses suggesting it was around **2 trillion parameters**[4][5], while others remained skeptical of such high numbers, noting that "active" parameters (the weights actually used per token) are often a more meaningful metric than total parameter counts for MoE models[6][1].

### Key Context
*   **Architecture:** Analysts widely believe Anthropic has shifted toward **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** architectures for its flagship models[4][9]. In such models, the "total" parameter count (the size of all experts combined) is much larger than the "active" parameter count (the subset of weights used to process any single token)[6][7].
*   **Official Stance:** Anthropic has consistently maintained that these details are proprietary[1][2].
*   **Model Lifecycle:** Claude Opus 4.6 is now considered a legacy model, having been succeeded by newer versions such as Claude Opus 5[10][11].
