### Key Context Regarding Benchmarks
*   **Official vs. Independent Results:** While 84.0% is the frequently cited figure for Gemini 2.5 Pro[1][2], official benchmarks can vary based on the specific testing harness, prompting techniques, and whether tools (like search or code execution) were permitted[3]. Some independent evaluations have reported slightly lower figures (typically 3–4 percentage points lower) than those reported by Google[3].
*   **Model Status:** Gemini 2.5 Pro was released in the first half of 2025[4][5] and is now considered an earlier-generation model compared to the current **Gemini 3.x** series (such as Gemini 3.1 Pro or 3.8 Flash), which are currently achieving significantly higher scores on the GPQA Diamond benchmark (often exceeding 94%)[6][7][8].
*   **Benchmark Difficulty:** GPQA Diamond is a highly challenging "Google-proof" dataset consisting of graduate-level questions in biology, physics, and chemistry[9][10][11]. Because it is designed to be difficult even for human experts (who typically score around 65–70%)[9][10][2], these scores are used as a primary metric for frontier reasoning capabilities[1][2].
