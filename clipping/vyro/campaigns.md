# Vyro — Platform Details & Campaigns

> Platform: [app.vyro.com](https://app.vyro.com)

## How Vyro Works
- Browse live campaigns from creators
- Each campaign has a pay rate ($ per 1,000 views) and minimum view threshold
- Submit your clips → they review → approved clips earn based on views
- **Views gained before submission do NOT count toward payouts**
